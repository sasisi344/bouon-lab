## 2. Financing & Tax Strategy (2026 Edition)
### 2.1 Loan Products Comparison
- **Reform Loan (Major Banks)**: 3.5% - 4.2%. Lowest rate but requires "renovation" status.
- **Instrument Loan (Orico/Jacks)**: 4.5% - 7.5%. Easier screening, but slightly higher rates.

### 2.2 Tax Benefits (Depreciation)
- **Business Use**: Can be depreciated as "Equipment (8-15 years)". 
- **Housing Loan Deduction**: Renovation costs over ¥1M (including soundproofing) may be eligible for a 0.7% tax credit if meeting "10-year loan" and "floor space" requirements.

---

## 3. ROI: Studio Rental vs. House + Loan
- **Studio Cost**: ~¥40,000/mo (Weekly x 3 sessions).
- **Loan Cost**: ~¥20,000/mo (payment) + ~¥30,000 (implied house rent diff).
- **Break-even**: Roughly 2.4 years. After this, owning a soundproof room is cheaper than renting studios due to residual asset value.
