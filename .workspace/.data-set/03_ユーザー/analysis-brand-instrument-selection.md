## 1. Instrument-Specific Requirements
- **Grand Piano**: Minimum 3.0 tatami for proper acoustics and weight distribution.
- **Saxophone**: High mid-frequency insulation (Dr-35-40).

## 2. Brand Comparisons
- Yamaha (Abitex): High insulation stability.
- Kawai (Nasal): Flexibility in custom sizing.
