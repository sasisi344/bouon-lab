# Soundproof Room Maintenance & Lifecycle Guide

Integrated data on cleaning, professional services, and lifespan management for soundproof rooms.

### 1. Cleaning & Maintenance Timeframes

| Task                   | Estimated Time | Notes                                      |
| :--------------------- | :------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| **Standard Cleaning**  | 0.5 - 1.5 hrs  | Dusting, floor wiping, daily hygiene.      |
| **Professional Clean** | 2.5 - 4 hrs    | Standard maintenance for panels and vents. |
| **Mold Remediation**   | 4 - 8 hrs      | Includes chemical treatment wait times.    |
| **AC/Vent Deep Clean** | +1 - 2 hrs     | Internal disassembly and washing.          |

**Downtime (Recovery Time):**

- **Standard:** Ready immediately (requires 30m ventilation).
- **Mold treatment:** 24 hours (Strict entry ban due to strong chemicals).
- **Instrument Maintenance:** 1 day (on-site) or 1-2 weeks (factory/off-site).

### 2. Professional Services & Costs

- **Manufacturers:** Yamaha (Abitex), Kawai (Nasal) for structural/acoustic performance.
- **Mold Specialists:** Hearts Clean, Mold Doctors (specialized in elimination/prevention).
- **General Cleaning:** Duskin, Bears, Osoji Honpo for surface maintenance.
- **Instrument Specialists:** Piano tuners/maintenance experts.

**Estimated Costs & Warnings:**

- **Mold Treatment:** ¥50k - ¥80k (1-2 mats), ¥75k - ¥110k (4.5 mats).
- **CRITICAL:** **Never wet-wipe** acoustic absorbent materials (e.g., glass wool).

### 3. Lifespan & Depreciation

**Statutory Life (Tax Purpose):**

- **Portable Units:** 8 years (treated as furniture/equipment).
- **Fixed Panels:** 15 years (building attachments).
- **Renovations:** 22 - 47 years (integrated building part).

**Physical Lifespan (Maintenance Points):**

- **Main Structure:** 30+ years with proper care.
- **Rubber Seals:** 5-10 years (cracking leads to severe sound leakage).
- **Ventilation/AC:** 8-15 years (mechanical/motor failure).
- **Relocation Limit:** 3-5 times (warping/screw-hole expansion degrades performance).

**Market Value Factors:**

- **15-year Mark:** Resale value drops as parts supply ends.
- **Odors/Hygiene:** Smoke, pet smells, or mold destroy resale value immediately.

### 4. Preservation Checklist

- **Ideal Humidity:** **40% - 60%** (Prevents mold and wood warping).
- **Daily Venting:** Force-ventilate for **15-30m** after every session.
- **10-year Refresh:** Replace all rubber seals and fans to restore "as-new" performance.
