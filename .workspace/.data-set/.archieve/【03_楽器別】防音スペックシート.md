### Core Principles for Implementation

#### 1. Dr-rating vs. Reality

- **Dr-30:** Conversations audible. Instruments reduced but night practice is difficult.
- **Dr-35:** Piano reduced to "low conversation" level. Suitable for daytime.
- **Dr-40:** Piano reduced to "whisper" level. Night practice feasible.
- **Critical:** Dr-ratings only measure air-borne sound. Bass/Vibration require separate damping.

#### 2. Space vs. Habitability

- **Movement Margin:** Add 10-20cm margin for bow/slide movement.
- **Climate Control:** Units < 1.2 mats trap heat rapidly. 1.2+ mats recommended for AC installation during long sessions.

#### 3. Vibration & Maintenance

- **Floor Impact:** Pedal/Kick shocks are the main source of neighbor complaints. Use vibration-damping rubber or floating stage.
- **Mold Risk:** High airtightness leads to moisture trap. Force-ventilate after use. No wet-wiping of inner panels.
