## 1. The Mass Law (質量則)
- Sound insulation is proportional to the logarithm of the weight (kg/m2) of the wall. 
- Doubling weight only increases TL (Transmission Loss) by ~5-6dB.

## 2. Common DIY Myths
- "Cardboard is enough": Wrong. Insufficient mass.
- "Egg crates": Absorption ONLY, zero insulation.
