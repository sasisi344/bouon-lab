# Project Skill: Movie Generation Rules (Short-Form / 15-Second Strategy)

This skill defines the visual, structural, and auditory standards for **15-second** promotional videos for BouonLab.
The goal is **"High-Trust Visuals + Precise Explanation + Immediate Reach"**.

---

Consistency across all videos is mandatory to build brand recognition.

## 🎞 2. The 15-Second Structure (Conversion Protocol)

A 15-second video is the "Sweet Spot" for algorithm engagement and curiosity induction.

1.  **0-3s: The Hook (Problem & Question)**
    *   *Visual*: High-impact macro shot or dramatic lighting reveal.
    *   *Captions*: Large, bold hook text (e.g., "Is Your Voice Leaking?").
    *   *VO*: Urgent but calm question.
2.  **3-12s: Core Value (The "How-to" Snippet)**
    *   *Visual*: 2 smooth panning shots showing the solution (Interior/Spec).
    *   *Captions*: Key facts (e.g., "Dr-60 Isolation", "Heat-Sync Ventilation").
    *   *VO*: Explaining the logic of the soundproof solution.
3.  **12-15s: The Reach (CTA)**
    *   *Visual*: Transition to "BouonLab" Logo screen in Midnight Blue.
    *   *Captions*: "Read Full Guide on Bio" or "Search [BouonLab]".
    *   *VO*: Branding sign-off.

## 🎙 3. Auditory & Text Layer Rules (Post-Production)

AI video generators often fail at text and audio. These must be layered during editing.

### A. Voiceover (VO) Standards
*   **Tone**: Calm, authoritative, sophisticated (Expert/Studio Engineer persona).
*   **Speed**: Deliberate and clear. Never rushed.
*   **Style**: Natural narration, avoiding "over-hyped" social media energy.

### B. Captions & Subtitles
*   **Font**: Bold Sans-serif (styled like the website).
*   **Location**: Lower-third or centered. Use high-contrast backgrounds (matte box).
*   **Rule**: One core message per scene. Less is more.

### C. Background Music (BGM)
*   **Genre**: Lo-fi, Ambient, Minimalist Techno (Deep bass).
*   **Volume**: Ducked significantly under the VO.

## 🛠 4. Master Prompt Template (English)

Use this base template for tools like Sora, Runway, Kling, or Luma. 

> **Prompt:**
> 15-second cinematic video. **Subject: [Subject]**. 
> **Visual Style**: Photorealistic architectural shot. Palette of **Midnight Blue** and **Slate Gray** with a soft **Amber LED accent glow**. High-end **acoustic panel** textures and a **matte-black studio setup**. 
> **Camera Motion**: Ultra-smooth slow panning or slow-zoom-in. Shallow depth of field with beautiful **bokeh**. 
> **Atmosphere**: Calm, professional, and silent. 4K, studio lighting, hyper-detailed, meditative mood.
