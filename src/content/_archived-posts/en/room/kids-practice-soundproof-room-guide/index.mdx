Home soundproof rooms are rapidly becoming a popular investment for children's piano practice and online learning in Japan.

However, from the perspective of a professional studio architect, buying a booth without understanding the <strong> physical risks of enclosing a child </strong> is extremely dangerous.

This guide moves beyond simple product comparisons. We explain how to clear three physical risks—floor load, CO2 concentration, and heatstroke—to build a safe "educational infrastructure" that nurtures your child's talent.

## Fatal Oversights in Piano Soundproofing

Focusing only on catalog specs like "Dr-value" can lead to irreversible failures. You must address these three physical threats first.

### Risk 1 : Structural Floor Collapse

A <strong> Dr-50 </strong> class soundproof room (1.5 tatami size) uses heavy materials like lead or double gypsum boards. The unit alone weighs <strong> 300kg to 400kg </strong>.
When you add an upright piano (200kg to 250kg) and the player, the total weight exceeds 600kg. This significantly surpasses the standard floor load capacity (180kg/㎡) of typical Japanese wooden houses or apartments.
Without <strong> weight distribution measures </strong> like base plates, there is a literal risk of the floor sinking or collapsing.

### Risk 2 : CO2 Concentration and Brain Fatigue

Soundproof rooms are extremely airtight. If a child studies or practices inside without proper ventilation, the CO2 concentration will exceed the focus-limiting threshold (1,000ppm) in just 20 to 30 minutes.
This causes drowsiness and headaches, defeating the purpose of a "study space."
The absolute requirement is a <strong> soundproof Lossnay ventilation system </strong> that operates continuously to exchange heat while providing fresh air.

### Risk 3 : Heatstroke from Insulation

Sound-absorbing and insulating materials also act as highly efficient thermal insulators. Body heat, lighting, and PC exhaust will cause the temperature in a 1 to 1.5 tatami space to skyrocket quickly.
Whether you can <strong> install a dedicated air conditioner </strong> is a lifeline for your child.
Relying on "portable spot coolers" is a fatal mistake. The noise from the compressor (over 50dB) and sound leakage from the exhaust duct destroy the very purpose of the soundproof room.
