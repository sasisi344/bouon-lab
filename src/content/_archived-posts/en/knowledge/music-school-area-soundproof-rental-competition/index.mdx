Competition for soundproof rentals is intensifying in music school areas in Japan. Soundproof rental properties near music schools are filling immediately when vacancies occur, and in urban areas, competition ratios are high, with cases of waiting several months not uncommon.

This article analyzes the current situation of competition for soundproof rentals in music school areas with data and explains the three reasons for rapid demand increase. We also publish vacancy status and pricing in major areas.

## What is Happening in Music School Areas? | Current State of "Soundproof Rental Competition Battle"

In music school areas, competition battles for soundproof rental properties occur daily. When vacancies appear, multiple applicants rush in, and properties fill immediately.

### Situation Where Soundproof Rentals Near Music Schools Fill Immediately

Soundproof rental properties near music schools fill within an average of one week when vacancies occur. In popular areas, cases of contracts being finalized within 3 days of vacancy are not uncommon.

According to a 2024 survey, the average vacancy period for soundproof rentals near music schools is approximately one-third of the national average. While average vacancy periods for regular rental properties are approximately one month, soundproof rentals near music schools fill in approximately 10 days.
