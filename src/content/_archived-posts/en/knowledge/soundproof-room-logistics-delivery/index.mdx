## Shipping Troubles: Cases and Prevention

Common problems:

### Trouble ①: Major delivery delays

<strong>Case</strong>

- <strong>Original promise:</strong> "4 weeks to delivery"
- <strong>Actual result:</strong> 2 months late
- <strong>Cause:</strong> Transporter scheduling conflict

<strong>Prevention</strong>

- <strong>Confirm delivery terms in writing</strong>
- <strong>Request delay handling specifics</strong>
- <strong>Obtain written timeline guarantee</strong>
- <strong>Regular progress updates</strong>

---

### Trouble ②: Transporter cancellation

<strong>Case</strong>

- <strong>Scheduled delivery:</strong> February 15
- <strong>One week prior:</strong> "Unable to proceed"
- <strong>Emergency backup contractor search</strong>

<strong>Prevention</strong>

- <strong>Request backup contractor standby</strong>
- <strong>Confirm 2 weeks before delivery</strong>
- <strong>Consider cancellation insurance</strong>

---

### Trouble ③: Property damage during transport

<strong>Case</strong>

- <strong>Door frame damaged</strong>
- <strong>Elevator wall scratched</strong>
- <strong>Ownership disputes</strong>

<strong>Prevention</strong>

- <strong>Pre-arrange damage insurance</strong>
- <strong>Verify carrier insurance</strong>
- <strong>Document before/after photos</strong>

---

### Trouble ④: Apartment access issues

<strong>Case</strong>

- <strong>Manufacturer:</strong> "Transport feasible"
- <strong>Building management:</strong> "Approval denied"
- <strong>Delivery cancelled days before</strong>

<strong>Prevention</strong>

- <strong>Consult management upfront (mandatory)</strong>
- <strong>Verify routes via building blueprints</strong>
- <strong>Confirm weight limits in writing</strong>
