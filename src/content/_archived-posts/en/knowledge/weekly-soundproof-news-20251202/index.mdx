### 5. Underground Parking to Soundproof Office: Rent Revenue 6x Increase

<strong>Source</strong>: [Underground Parking to Soundproof Office, Revenue 6x](https://nfm.nikkeibp.co.jp/atcl/news/21/00001/07482/)

A case of converting urban station-area underground parking into a new business model is attracting attention.

#### Background

<strong>Station-Area Parking Decline</strong>:

- Increasing trend of driving to large stores, reducing station-area parking use
- Challenging operations as parking facilities
- <strong>Renovation anticipating new demand converted parking to soundproof office</strong>

#### Business Model Advantages

<strong>Location Utilization</strong>:

- Immediately adjacent to train stations
- High convenience
- Inherent soundproofing from underground location

<strong>Use Cases</strong>:

- Shared offices
- Rental spaces
- Streaming booths

<strong>Operating System</strong>:

- Operating until near last train (addressing nighttime use needs)
- Electronic locks + unmanned system (efficiency)

<strong>Revenue Increased 6x</strong>:

- Parking: ¥10,000 (~$67)/month per space
- Soundproof office: ¥60,000 (~$400)/month per room (same space as one parking spot)

#### Business Insight

This model has potential to <strong>create new economic zones in regional city station areas</strong>.

In an era of parking oversupply, conversion to soundproof offices is a promising solution.

<strong>Applicability</strong>:

- Station-area parking in regional cities
- Parking at suburban large commercial facilities
- Unused underground spaces

A noteworthy new business model leveraging soundproofing and location.
