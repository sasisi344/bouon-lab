## Overview

Asia's compact soundproof booth demand shows <strong>annual growth of 8-12%</strong> driven by streaming and creator economy with continued hybrid work. Among these, <strong>China, Korea, and Vietnam</strong> are experiencing accelerated growth riding the waves of "streaming × e-commerce/mobile advancement."

This article analyzes country-specific market opportunities and proposes strategic expansion directions for Japanese manufacturers.

## Asia's Major Markets: Soundproof Booth Market Scale and Growth Rates

### China: Massive Streaming Population, Expanding Office Demand

<strong>Market Scale and Foundation</strong>
- Live streaming users: <strong>833 million</strong> (75% of internet users)
- Digital population: 1.37 billion
- Urbanization rate: ~65% and rapidly rising

<strong>Demand Drivers</strong>
- Rapid expansion of live commerce and short video platforms
- Increasing noise complaints in urban residential buildings
- Corporate office soundproof booth adoption

<strong>Growth Outlook</strong>
- Annual rate: <strong>+10-14%</strong> (2025-2030)
- Particularly "broad and shallow" penetration to general users
- Mainstream adoption for office and educational use

<strong>Impact on Japan</strong>
- Individual booth market success depends on volume × price range optimization
- Easy assembly and heat management are selling points

### South Korea: K-POP and Streaming Culture, Strong Quality Focus

<strong>Market Scale and Foundation</strong>
- Live streaming market CAGR: <strong>~26%</strong> (2025-2035 outlook)
- K-POP idol fan activities ("oshi-katsuo") are culturalized
- Gaming and educational streaming also thriving

<strong>Demand Drivers</strong>
- Fan activities, gaming, educational streaming culture
- Consumers prioritizing high quality and aesthetics
- Rising incomes among content creator class

<strong>Growth Outlook</strong>
- Annual rate: <strong>+9-12%</strong> (primarily individual soundproof booths)
- Higher unit price demand for <strong>high-performance/design</strong> solutions
- Market maturity monetizing otaku culture

<strong>Impact on Japan</strong>
- Design and acoustic quality differentiation effective
- Competitive advantage through lighting, ventilation, wiring completeness

### Vietnam: 75% of Population Active on SNS, Live Commerce Established

<strong>Market Scale and Foundation</strong>
- Active SNS users: <strong>76.2 million</strong> (75% of population)
- E-commerce acceleration, TikTok Shop adoption
- Mobile-first, high SNS literacy

<strong>Demand Drivers</strong>
- Live commerce (live streaming sales) establishment
- Solo entrepreneurs and retailers setting up streaming
- YouTube and side-business entrepreneurial aspirations

<strong>Growth Outlook</strong>
- Annual rate: <strong>+12-16%</strong> (high-growth market)
- <strong>Simple booths</strong> in entry to mid-price ranges showing growth
- Government digital transformation support driving growth

<strong>Impact on Japan</strong>
- Price competitiveness prioritized, but quality reputation important
- Video demonstrations of noise reduction enhance recognition

### Japan: Mature Market with Soundproof Rental Differentiation

<strong>Market Scale and Foundation</strong>
- VTuber, ASMR, and telework demand steady
- Accumulated soundproof rental model expertise
- High technical quality and service capability

<strong>Demand Drivers</strong>
- Increasing VTuber and ASMR creator numbers
- Work-from-home establishment driving home environment investment
- Soundproof rental awareness penetration

<strong>Growth Outlook</strong>
- Annual rate: <strong>+6-9%</strong> (mature × steady)
- Bipolarization of rental vs. compact booths progressing
- No saturation signs currently visible

## Country-Specific Approach Recommendations

### China: Volume × Price Range Optimization Strategy

<strong>Measures</strong>
- Emphasize foldability and easy assembly
- Promote heat management (summer cooling efficiency)
- Multiple price tier lineup
- Listing on major e-commerce platforms (Alibaba, JD.com, etc.)

<strong>Expected Market Segments</strong>
- Individual creators: +12% annual growth
- Corporate offices: +8% annual growth

### South Korea: High Sound Isolation × Aesthetics Strategy

<strong>Measures</strong>
- <strong>High sound isolation × aesthetics</strong>: D-50+ with differentiation through lighting, ventilation, and wiring "completeness"
- Comprehensive lighting equipment, smart features
- Design award recognition for awareness building
- Collaboration with local influencers

<strong>Expected Market Segments</strong>
- K-POP fan streamers: Boom-stage growth
- Gaming streamers: Steady demand

### Vietnam: Entry to Mid-Range Price × Video Appeal

<strong>Measures</strong>
- <strong>Entry to mid-range simple booths</strong> + <strong>noise/echo demonstration video</strong> appeal
- Local SNS influencer collaboration
- Expansion via Shopee, Lazada
- Localization (Vietnamese language support)

<strong>Expected Market Segments</strong>
- Retailer live commerce: Rapid growth
- YouTuber side-businesses: Steady growth

### Japan: Soundproof Rental Model Export Hybrid

<strong>Measures</strong>
- Purchase vs. rental vs. lease comparison guidance
- Soundproof rental partnership building
- VTuber/ASMR feature standardization

## Summary: Asia Soundproof Booth Market Expansion Strategy

Japan's soundproofing technology and service capabilities have sufficient competitiveness in Asian markets. The critical success factor is <strong>region-specific needs understanding and phased market development</strong>.

<strong>Country Selection Priority</strong>
1. <strong>Immediate entry</strong>: Vietnam (favorable growth rate and entry barriers)
2. <strong>Medium-term entry</strong>: South Korea (differentiation possible through design and quality)
3. <strong>Long-term expansion</strong>: China (large scale but watch for intensifying competition)

The next five years will be critical for Japanese manufacturers' Asia expansion. Early participation capturing first-mover advantage and differentiation strategies leveraging regional characteristics will determine success.
