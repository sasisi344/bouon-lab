## Overview

Asia's compact soundproof booth demand shows <strong>annual growth of 8-12%</strong> driven by streaming and creator economy with continued hybrid work. Among these, <strong>China, Korea, and Vietnam</strong> are experiencing accelerated growth riding the waves of "streaming × e-commerce/mobile advancement."

This article analyzes country-specific market opportunities and proposes strategic expansion directions for Japanese manufacturers.

## Asia's Major Markets: Soundproof Booth Market Scale and Growth Rates

### China: Massive Streaming Population, Expanding Office Demand

<strong>Market Scale and Foundation</strong>
- <strong>Live streaming users:</strong> <strong>833 million (75% of internet users)</strong>
- <strong>Digital population:</strong> 1.37 billion
- <strong>Urbanization rate:</strong> ~65% and rapidly rising

<strong>Demand Drivers</strong>
- <strong>Rapid expansion of live commerce and short video platforms</strong>
- <strong>Increasing noise complaints in urban residential buildings</strong>
- <strong>Corporate office soundproof booth adoption</strong>

<strong>Growth Outlook</strong>
- <strong>Annual rate:</strong> <strong>+10-14% (2025-2030)</strong>
- <strong>Particularly "broad and shallow" penetration to general users</strong>
- <strong>Mainstream adoption for office and educational use</strong>

<strong>Impact on Japan</strong>
- <strong>Individual booth market success depends on volume × price range optimization</strong>
- <strong>Easy assembly and heat management are selling points</strong>

### South Korea: K-POP and Streaming Culture, Strong Quality Focus

<strong>Market Scale and Foundation</strong>
- <strong>Live streaming market CAGR:</strong> <strong>~26% (2025-2035 outlook)</strong>
- <strong>K-POP idol fan activities ("oshi-katsuo") are culturalized</strong>
- <strong>Gaming and educational streaming also thriving</strong>

<strong>Demand Drivers</strong>
- <strong>Fan activities, gaming, educational streaming culture</strong>
- <strong>Consumers prioritizing high quality and aesthetics</strong>
- <strong>Rising incomes among content creator class</strong>

<strong>Growth Outlook</strong>
- <strong>Annual rate:</strong> <strong>+9-12% (primarily individual soundproof booths)</strong>
- <strong>Higher unit price demand for <strong>high-performance/design solutions</strong></strong>
- <strong>Market maturity monetizing otaku culture</strong>

<strong>Impact on Japan</strong>
- <strong>Design and acoustic quality differentiation effective</strong>
- <strong>Competitive advantage through lighting, ventilation, wiring completeness</strong>

### Vietnam: 75% of Population Active on SNS, Live Commerce Established

<strong>Market Scale and Foundation</strong>
- <strong>Active SNS users:</strong> <strong>76.2 million (75% of population)</strong>
- <strong>E-commerce acceleration, TikTok Shop adoption</strong>
- <strong>Mobile-first, high SNS literacy</strong>

<strong>Demand Drivers</strong>
- <strong>Live commerce (live streaming sales) establishment</strong>
- <strong>Solo entrepreneurs and retailers setting up streaming</strong>
- <strong>YouTube and side-business entrepreneurial aspirations</strong>

<strong>Growth Outlook</strong>
- <strong>Annual rate:</strong> <strong>+12-16% (high-growth market)</strong>
- <strong>Simple booths in entry to mid-price ranges showing growth</strong>
- <strong>Government digital transformation support driving growth</strong>

<strong>Impact on Japan</strong>
- <strong>Price competitiveness prioritized, but quality reputation important</strong>
- <strong>Video demonstrations of noise reduction enhance recognition</strong>

### Japan: Mature Market with Soundproof Rental Differentiation

<strong>Market Scale and Foundation</strong>
- <strong>VTuber, ASMR, and telework demand steady</strong>
- <strong>Accumulated soundproof rental model expertise</strong>
- <strong>High technical quality and service capability</strong>

<strong>Demand Drivers</strong>
- <strong>Increasing VTuber and ASMR creator numbers</strong>
- <strong>Work-from-home establishment driving home environment investment</strong>
- <strong>Soundproof rental awareness penetration</strong>

<strong>Growth Outlook</strong>
- <strong>Annual rate:</strong> <strong>+6-9% (mature × steady)</strong>
- <strong>Bipolarization of rental vs. compact booths progressing</strong>
- <strong>No saturation signs currently visible</strong>

## Country-Specific Approach Recommendations

### China: Volume × Price Range Optimization Strategy

<strong>Measures</strong>
- <strong>Emphasize foldability and easy assembly</strong>
- <strong>Promote heat management (summer cooling efficiency)</strong>
- <strong>Multiple price tier lineup</strong>
- <strong>Listing on major e-commerce platforms (Alibaba, JD.com, etc.)</strong>

<strong>Expected Market Segments</strong>
- <strong>Individual creators:</strong> +12% annual growth
- <strong>Corporate offices:</strong> +8% annual growth

### South Korea: High Sound Isolation × Aesthetics Strategy

<strong>Measures</strong>
- <strong>High sound isolation × aesthetics:</strong> D-50+ with differentiation through lighting, ventilation, and wiring "completeness"
- <strong>Comprehensive lighting equipment, smart features</strong>
- <strong>Design award recognition for awareness building</strong>
- <strong>Collaboration with local influencers</strong>

<strong>Expected Market Segments</strong>
- <strong>K-POP fan streamers:</strong> Boom-stage growth
- <strong>Gaming streamers:</strong> Steady demand

### Vietnam: Entry to Mid-Range Price × Video Appeal

<strong>Measures</strong>
- <strong>Entry to mid-range simple booths + <strong>noise/echo demonstration video appeal</strong></strong>
- <strong>Local SNS influencer collaboration</strong>
- <strong>Expansion via Shopee, Lazada</strong>
- <strong>Localization (Vietnamese language support)</strong>

<strong>Expected Market Segments</strong>
- <strong>Retailer live commerce:</strong> Rapid growth
- <strong>YouTuber side-businesses:</strong> Steady growth

### Japan: Soundproof Rental Model Export Hybrid

<strong>Measures</strong>
- <strong>Purchase vs. rental vs. lease comparison guidance</strong>
- <strong>Soundproof rental partnership building</strong>
- <strong>VTuber/ASMR feature standardization</strong>

## Summary: Asia Soundproof Booth Market Expansion Strategy

Japan's soundproofing technology and service capabilities have sufficient competitiveness in Asian markets. The critical success factor is <strong>region-specific needs understanding and phased market development</strong>.

<strong>Country Selection Priority</strong>
1. <strong>Immediate entry</strong>: Vietnam (favorable growth rate and entry barriers)
2. <strong>Medium-term entry</strong>: South Korea (differentiation possible through design and quality)
3. <strong>Long-term expansion</strong>: China (large scale but watch for intensifying competition)

The next five years will be critical for Japanese manufacturers' Asia expansion. Early participation capturing first-mover advantage and differentiation strategies leveraging regional characteristics will determine success.
