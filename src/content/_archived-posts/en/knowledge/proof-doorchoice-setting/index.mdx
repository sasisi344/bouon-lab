"Installed soundproof room but sound leaks through door..." "Sound completely escapes through windows..." Have these concerns?

Actually, doors and windows are soundproof room weak points. No matter how excellent wall sound insulation is, inadequate opening countermeasures halve effectiveness. This article explains soundproof door and double window effects through selection methods and DIY improvements.

## Why Are Doors and Windows Soundproofing Weak Points?

Why do doors and windows leak sound even in soundproof rooms?

### 3 Reasons Doors/Windows Leak Sound

<strong>1. Structural Gaps Exist</strong>
- Inevitable gaps between door and frame
- Windows can't seal completely either
- Even 1mm gaps leak sound

<strong>Sound Leakage Principle:</strong>
```
Wall sound insulation: D-60 (excellent)
Door gap: D-20 (insufficient)
→ Actual whole-room performance: ~D-25
```

Weakest point determines overall performance.

<strong>2. Thin, Lightweight Materials</strong>
- Doors: Thinner than walls
- Windows: Glass thinner than wall materials
- Less mass = less sound blocking

<strong>3. Vibration Transmission</strong>
- Doors/windows vibrate from sound
- Vibration transmits sound to other side
- Particularly problematic for low frequencies

## Soundproof Door Solutions

### Types of Soundproof Doors

<strong>Studio-Grade Soundproof Doors:</strong>
- Performance: D-40~60
- Cost: 150,000-600,000 yen
- Weight: 40-80kg
- Thickness: 50-80mm

<strong>Enhanced Regular Doors:</strong>
- Performance: D-30~40
- Cost: 50,000-150,000 yen
- Weight: 25-40kg
- Thickness: 40-50mm

### Door Seal Solutions

<strong>Magnetic Seals:</strong>
- Cost: 10,000-30,000 yen
- Installation: Moderate difficulty
- Effectiveness: +10~15dB improvement

<strong>Compression Seals:</strong>
- Cost: 8,000-20,000 yen
- Installation: Easy (DIY)
- Effectiveness: +8~12dB improvement

<strong>Door Sweep:</strong>
- Cost: 3,000-8,000 yen
- Installation: Very easy
- Effectiveness: +5~8dB improvement

### DIY Door Improvements

<strong>Budget: Under 20,000 yen</strong>
1. Weatherstrip seal: 2,000-5,000 yen
2. Door sweep: 3,000-8,000 yen
3. Additional mass (MDF panel): 5,000-10,000 yen

<strong>Expected:</strong> +10~15dB improvement

## Installation Priorities

### Which to Address First?

<strong>Priority 1: Door Seals</strong>
- Biggest impact for lowest cost
- DIY-friendly
- Immediate results
- Start here always

<strong>Priority 2: Window Treatment</strong>
- If windows are significant leak source
- Curtains: Immediate, affordable
- Double glazing: Long-term investment

<strong>Priority 3: Complete Door Replacement</strong>
- If seals insufficient
- Major upgrade for persistent leakage
- Professional installation required
