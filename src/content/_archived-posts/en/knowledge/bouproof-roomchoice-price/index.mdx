## Property Viewing Checklist

### Soundproofing Performance Test

<strong>During Viewing:</strong>
1. Bring portable speaker
2. Play music inside soundproof room
3. Listen from outside and adjacent units
4. Verify actual sound insulation

<strong>Sound Test Protocol:</strong>
- <strong>Play at normal practice volume</strong>
- <strong>Test various frequencies (highs, mids, lows)</strong>
- <strong>Check multiple locations outside</strong>
- <strong>Ask to hear from hallway/adjacent room if possible</strong>

### Physical Inspection

<strong>Soundproof Room Interior:</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Size adequate for your instruments</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Ceiling height sufficient</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Good condition (no damage)</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Proper lighting</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Adequate outlets</strong>

<strong>Door/Seals:</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Opens/closes smoothly</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Seals intact (no cracks)</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Proper airtight closure</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Lock functions correctly</strong>

<strong>Ventilation:</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Fan operates quietly</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Adequate airflow</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Filters clean</strong>
- <strong>[ ] Controls accessible</strong>

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Pitfall 1: Assuming 24-Hour Practice

<strong>Reality:</strong> Many soundproof rentals have time restrictions
<strong>Solution:</strong> Verify exact permissible hours before signing

### Pitfall 2: Not Testing Actual Soundproofing

<strong>Reality:</strong> D-values don't tell whole story
<strong>Solution:</strong> Always test with actual instruments during viewing

### Pitfall 3: Ignoring Ventilation Adequacy

<strong>Reality:</strong> Many rooms get uncomfortably hot
<strong>Solution:</strong> Verify climate control during summer if possible

### Pitfall 4: Overlooking Total Costs

<strong>Reality:</strong> Initial costs can exceed 1 million yen
<strong>Solution:</strong> Budget comprehensively including all upfront fees
