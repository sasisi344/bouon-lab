## Essential Equipment Verification

### Soundproofing Specifications

<strong>Must Verify:</strong>
- [ ] Actual D-value rating
- [ ] Measurement method used
- [ ] Performance documentation
- [ ] Recent acoustic test results

<strong>Questions to Ask:</strong>
- "What's the exact D-value?"
- "Can I see test results?"
- "Has performance been verified recently?"

### Ventilation System

<strong>Critical Points:</strong>
- Fan capacity (m³/h)
- Noise level during operation
- Filter maintenance responsibility
- Upgrade possibilities

<strong>Red Flags:</strong>
- No ventilation system
- Very noisy fans
- Poor air circulation

### Climate Control

<strong>Essential Checks:</strong>
- AC unit capacity
- Summer temperature management
- Winter heating adequacy
- Humidity control options

<strong>Recommendations:</strong>
- Test AC during viewing
- Ask about summer usage
- Verify electrical capacity

## Property Viewing Checklist

### Soundproofing Performance Test

<strong>During Viewing:</strong>
1. Bring portable speaker
2. Play music inside soundproof room
3. Listen from outside and adjacent units
4. Verify actual sound insulation

<strong>Sound Test Protocol:</strong>
- Play at normal practice volume
- Test various frequencies (highs, mids, lows)
- Check multiple locations outside
- Ask to hear from hallway/adjacent room if possible

### Physical Inspection

<strong>Soundproof Room Interior:</strong>
- [ ] Size adequate for your instruments
- [ ] Ceiling height sufficient
- [ ] Good condition (no damage)
- [ ] Proper lighting
- [ ] Adequate outlets

<strong>Door/Seals:</strong>
- [ ] Opens/closes smoothly
- [ ] Seals intact (no cracks)
- [ ] Proper airtight closure
- [ ] Lock functions correctly

<strong>Ventilation:</strong>
- [ ] Fan operates quietly
- [ ] Adequate airflow
- [ ] Filters clean
- [ ] Controls accessible

### Building Considerations

<strong>Common Areas:</strong>
- Instrument carrying route
- Elevator size
- Parking availability
- Storage rooms

<strong>Neighbor Situation:</strong>
- Who lives in adjacent units
- Building atmosphere
- Community rules
- Past complaint history

## Contract Negotiation

### Initial Costs

<strong>Typical Breakdown:</strong>
- Deposit: 2-3 months rent
- Key money: 1-2 months
- Agency fee: 1 month
- Fire insurance: 20,000-30,000 yen/year
- Cleaning fee: 50,000-80,000 yen

<strong>Negotiation Points:</strong>
- Request key money reduction
- Negotiate deposit terms
- Ask about move-in specials
- Inquire about renewal fee waivers

### Special Clauses

<strong>Practice Hours:</strong>
- Get exact hours in writing
- Understand any restrictions
- Verify enforcement policy

<strong>Soundproof Room Use:</strong>
- Confirm included in rent
- Verify exclusive use
- Check maintenance terms

<strong>Restoration Obligations:</strong>
- Normal wear vs. damage
- Cleaning requirements
- Equipment replacement responsibility

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Pitfall 1: Assuming 24-Hour Practice

<strong>Reality:</strong> Many soundproof rentals have time restrictions
<strong>Solution:</strong> Verify exact permissible hours before signing

### Pitfall 2: Not Testing Actual Soundproofing

<strong>Reality:</strong> D-values don't tell whole story
<strong>Solution:</strong> Always test with actual instruments during viewing

### Pitfall 3: Ignoring Ventilation Adequacy

<strong>Reality:</strong> Many rooms get uncomfortably hot
<strong>Solution:</strong> Verify climate control during summer if possible

### Pitfall 4: Overlooking Total Costs

<strong>Reality:</strong> Initial costs can exceed 1 million yen
<strong>Solution:</strong> Budget comprehensively including all upfront fees
