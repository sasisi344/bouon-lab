## Points for Lossnay Installation and Operation

Let's explain points for effectively operating Lossnay.

### Installation Position: Wall Side or Ceiling Away from Sound Source

Lossnay installation position is important: <strong>away from sound source</strong>.

<strong>Recommended Installation Position</strong>

- At least 1.5m from sound source
- Install on wall side (ceiling-embedded type also possible)
- Separate intake and exhaust openings

<strong>Positions to Avoid</strong>

- Near sound source
- In front of microphone
- Near instruments

### Do Not Make Intake/Exhaust Paths Straight (Soundproof with Bent Ducts)

It's important that intake/exhaust paths are <strong>not straight</strong>.

<strong>Recommended Design</strong>

- Create 90-degree or greater bends
- Combine multiple bends
- Soundproof with bent ducts

<strong>Effect</strong>

- Straight path: Large sound leakage
- Bent path: Sound leakage attenuated by −10dB or more

### Strengthen Soundproofing Performance by Adding Sound-Absorbing Wool Inside Ducts

Adding sound-absorbing wool inside ducts can further strengthen soundproofing performance.

<strong>Addition Method</strong>

- Attach sound-absorbing wool to duct inner walls
- Thickness: 50mm+ recommended
- Materials: Glass wool, urethane foam

<strong>Effect</strong>

- Sound leakage: Further −5 to 10dB attenuation
- Ventilation performance: Maintained
- Soundproofing performance: Maintains D-35 to 45

### Maintenance: Filter Replacement 1-2 Times Per Year, Internal Cleaning to Maintain Performance

Regular maintenance maintains Lossnay performance.

<strong>Maintenance Items</strong>

- Filter replacement: 1-2 times per year
- Internal cleaning: Once every 6 months
- Operation check: Monthly

<strong>Effect</strong>

- Maintains ventilation performance
- Prevents sound leakage
- Extends lifespan
