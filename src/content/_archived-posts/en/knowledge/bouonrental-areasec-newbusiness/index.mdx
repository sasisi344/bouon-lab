## Overview

With rapid proliferation of streaming culture, the soundproof rental market has expanded from traditional music enthusiasts to YouTubers, VTubers, and gaming streamers. However, the current situation of <strong>78.7% supply concentrated in the Kanto region</strong> is also noteworthy as a <strong>new angle for regional revitalization</strong>.

This article analyzes supply-demand balance between urban and rural areas based on latest data, proposing rural creator recruitment strategies.
