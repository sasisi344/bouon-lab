## What is MUSISION? Pioneer of Soundproof Rentals in Japan

### Operating Company and Business Overview

<strong>MUSISION</strong> is a soundproof rental apartment series developed and operated by Livlan Co., Ltd.

As of November 2024, the brand has expanded to <strong>41 buildings with 828 units</strong>, establishing itself as a pioneer in the soundproof rental industry in Japan.

## MUSISION's Soundproofing Performance: Peace of Mind with D-Value Disclosure

### What is D-Value?

<strong>D-value</strong> is an index indicating sound isolation performance. Higher numbers mean better sound blocking capability.

<strong>D-Value Reference Guide</strong>:

| D-Value | Isolation Performance | Instrument Practice Feasibility |
|---------|----------------------|--------------------------------|
| D-35 | Neighbor's conversation faintly audible | Piano/strings difficult |
| D-40 | Neighbor's loud voice/music slightly audible | Acoustic instruments time-dependent |
| D-50 | Neighbor's sounds barely audible | Most instruments playable |
| <strong>D-60+</strong> | <strong>Neighbor's sounds completely inaudible</strong> | <strong>24-hour practice possible</strong> |

### MUSISION's Soundproofing Performance

MUSISION focuses on properties with <strong>D-50 to D-60+ level high isolation performance</strong>.

<strong>Main Soundproofing Features</strong>:

- <strong>Double-glazed windows</strong>: Significantly reduces external noise
- <strong>Floating floor structure</strong>: Blocks vibrations from floors
- <strong>High-isolation walls</strong>: Thick walls with specialized materials
- <strong>Soundproof doors</strong>: Prevents sound leakage through openings

These designs enable 24-hour practice even with loud instruments like contrabass.
