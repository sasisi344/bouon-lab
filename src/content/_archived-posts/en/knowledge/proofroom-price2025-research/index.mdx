Since 2020, soundproof room prices have quietly yet steadily risen. Backgrounds include international construction material inflation and chronic labor shortages driving installation cost increases.

Particularly following 2021's "wood shock," key materials—lumber, gypsum board, acoustic absorbers—increased 20-30%, while craftsman labor rates rose 2-3% annually. Consequently, unit soundproof rooms installable for 1,000,000 yen in 2020 now average ~1,300,000 yen in 2025.

This article analyzes past five years (2020-2025) price trends from data, organizing material inflation and labor cost increase realities numerically. Subsequently, examining design, procurement, and construction phases considers what innovations cost optimization requires going forward.

## Current Situation: 2020→2025 Developments

Since 2020, <strong>construction material prices and labor costs</strong> for soundproof room manufacturing/installation simultaneously rose, elevating total costs to higher plateaus. Public indicators showing construction price/work cost trends indicate material price indices <strong>rose ~30-40% from early 2021</strong>, while public work design labor rates increased <strong>~23% from 2021</strong>.

Consequently, total construction costs combining materials, labor, and overhead increased an estimated +25-30% range, with soundproof room (unit/interior types) quotes experiencing nearly equivalent pressure.

This "dual increase pressure (materials + labor)" shows minimal signs of short-term reversal. As of 2025, material supply-demand remains generally "balanced~stable," while price levels persist at elevated positions.

## Causes: Why Increases Occurred

<strong>Event Factors (2021 Shock):</strong>
"Wood shock" caused <strong>sudden lumber price surges</strong>. Import lumber average prices recorded <strong>+100%+ year-over-year in 2021</strong> (lumber +106%, plywood +51%, laminated lumber +114%—all December year-over-year), directly impacting interior soundproofing using wood framing, plywood, composite materials.

<strong>Structural Factor ①(Energy, Raw Materials, Logistics):</strong>
Glass wool and other <strong>fiber-based acoustic materials</strong> depend on energy-intensive manufacturing/transport costs. Major domestic insulation/acoustic material manufacturers implemented <strong>25-35% price increases</strong> in stages 2022-2025 (example: January 2023 uniform +25% announcement, September 2025 some products +35% revisions).

<strong>Structural Factor ②(Gypsum Board Continuous Revisions):</strong>
Core lightweight partition material <strong>gypsum board</strong> also saw +30% re-increase announcements and multiple price revisions. Essential for interior insulation/mass provision, directly affecting soundproof room costs.

<strong>Structural Factor ③(Labor Costs, Personnel Acquisition):</strong>
Construction skilled labor wage equivalents using <strong>design labor rates +22.9% from 2021</strong>. Additionally, nationwide <strong>minimum wage continuous increases</strong> (2025 national average <strong>1,121 yen</strong>, +66 yen year-over-year) raise base costs. Amid labor shortages and aging, "human-intensive processes"—installation, delivery, nighttime construction—saw unit price elevation.
