Are you wondering whether to buy a Danbotchi or build your own soundproof room?

For those wanting to set up streaming or recording environments, choosing between commercial products and DIY is an important decision. There are multiple points to compare: price, performance, installation time, rental compatibility, and more.

This article organizes <strong>decision criteria with data</strong> to help you make the optimal choice for your needs.

## Introduction: Why More People Are Struggling with Commercial vs DIY

In recent years, more people are struggling with whether to "buy a Danbotchi or build their own." The background includes the following reasons.

### Increase in Streamers and Creators

Streaming activities where sound quality is important, such as VTubers, game commentary, and podcasts, are rapidly increasing. Demand for setting up streaming environments at home is rising, increasing interest in soundproofing measures.

### Diversification of Options

Previously, it was a choice between professional unit-type soundproof rooms (¥1,000,000+) or doing nothing. However, now there are more intermediate options such as <strong>Danbotchi (¥100,000-150,000)</strong> and <strong>DIY soundproof rooms (¥20,000-50,000)</strong>.

### Complexity of Decision Criteria

There are multiple points to compare beyond just price: <strong>performance, installation time, rental compatibility, maintenance</strong>, and more. You need to understand the advantages and disadvantages of each to make the optimal choice for yourself.

### What is Danbotchi?

<strong>Danbotchi</strong> is an assembly-type simple soundproof booth developed by Yamaha. It uses cardboard material in a lightweight design that can be assembled by one person, even a woman.

<strong>Main Features</strong>
- <strong>Assembly-type, no construction needed</strong>
- <strong>Easy restoration (can be disassembled and removed)</strong>
- <strong>Optimal for streaming and calls</strong>
- <strong>Manufacturer warranty included</strong>

<strong>Size Variations</strong>
- <strong>Wide:</strong> 120cm wide × 90cm deep × 190cm tall (¥100,000-120,000)
- <strong>Tall:</strong> 120cm wide × 90cm deep × 210cm tall (¥130,000-150,000)

### What is a DIY Soundproof Room?

A <strong>DIY soundproof room</strong> is a soundproof space built by purchasing materials yourself and constructing with DIY. Methods using closets are common.

<strong>Main Features</strong>
- <strong>Achievable at low cost</strong>
- <strong>Can freely design size</strong>
- <strong>Can improve gradually</strong>
- <strong>Rental-compatible depending on construction method</strong>

<strong>Representative Construction Methods</strong>
- <strong>Closet soundproof room:</strong> Utilize existing closet
- <strong>Simple soundproof booth:</strong> Create frame with pipe scaffolding
- <strong>Oshiire soundproof room:</strong> Utilize Japanese-style closet
