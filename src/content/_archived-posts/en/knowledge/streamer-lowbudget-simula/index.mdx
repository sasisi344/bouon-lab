Want to start streaming but don't want to disturb family. But soundproof rooms are expensive... I completely understand this concern.

Actually, streaming environments can be created starting from 30,000 yen. This article introduces "immediate actionable soundproofing" by budget tier.

## What Soundproofing Level Does Streaming Need?

First, know the soundproofing performance streaming requires. Technically measured in "D-values."

<strong>D-45 approximately sufficient for streaming.</strong> This means "conversation from next room barely audible" level. Perfect silence isn't necessary—streaming succeeds without it.

Incidentally, premium soundproof rooms rate D-60+, but whether you need this depends on use. Unless late-night shouting in game streaming, it might be over-spec for normal streaming.

## 30,000 Yen Budget Soundproofing Strategy

### Strategic Acoustic Panel Placement

Most effective 30,000 yen use: smart absorber deployment.

<strong>Materials:</strong>
- Acoustic panels (Amazon, etc.): ~15,000 yen
- Heavy blackout curtains: ~8,000 yen
- Gap sealing tape: ~2,000 yen
- Remaining: Miscellaneous/adjustments

Absorbers don't "reduce volume" but "reduce echo." For streaming, reduced echo dramatically transforms audio quality.

<strong>Effective Placement:</strong>

<strong>Priority 1: Behind Microphone</strong>
- Panels directly behind mic
- Prevents echo return
- Most impactful placement

<strong>Priority 2: Side Walls</strong>
- Panels on walls mic faces
- Reduces first reflections
- Second most impactful

<strong>Priority 3: Ceiling (If Budget Allows)</strong>
- Above microphone position
- Reduces overhead reflections

<strong>Expected Results:</strong>
- Echo reduction: ~50%
- Audio clarity: Noticeably improved
- Neighbor disturbance: Slightly reduced (not major)

<strong>Suitable For:</strong> Daytime streaming, moderate volumes

### 30,000 Yen Implementation Example

<strong>Purchase List:</strong>
1. Acoustic foam 24-pack: 12,000 yen
2. Heavy curtains (2 panels): 7,000 yen
3. Door seal strips: 2,000 yen
4. Desk pad (keyboard noise): 3,000 yen
5. Cable ties/mounting: 2,000 yen
6. Hygrometer: 1,500 yen
7. Remaining: 2,500 yen reserve

<strong>Installation:</strong>
- Wall panels: 2 hours
- Curtains: 30 minutes
- Door seals: 1 hour
- Total time: ~4 hours

## Free and Low-Cost Additions

### Free Improvements

<strong>Closet Conversion:</strong>
- Cost: Free
- Method: Record in clothing-filled closet
- Effect: Natural sound absorption
- Limitation: Cramped, poor ventilation

<strong>Blanket Fort:</strong>
- Cost: Minimal (existing blankets)
- Method: Drape thick blankets around recording area
- Effect: Reduces echo significantly
- Limitation: Not aesthetically pleasing, temporary

<strong>Software Filters:</strong>
- OBS noise suppression: Free
- Audacity noise reduction: Free
- Effect: 50-70% noise improvement
