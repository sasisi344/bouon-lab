## Overview

Thunder noise exceeds 100dB, significantly disrupting concentration and sleep. <strong>Understanding soundproofing performance and implementing proper home design and environmental improvements</strong> are essential for securing work-from-home time and quality sleep. This article organizes building structure, windows, room soundproofing, shutters, and costs to provide practical guidance.

## Thunder Noise and Sound Pressure Basics

First, let's understand thunder loudness and comfortable acoustic environments for humans.

<strong>Thunder Sound Pressure Levels</strong>
- <strong>Direct lightning strike:</strong> <strong>100-120dB (jet engine, concert level)</strong>
- <strong>Nearby lightning:</strong> <strong>80-100dB (train passage, loud traffic)</strong>

<strong>Comfortable Sleep and Work Environment</strong>
- <strong>Ideal sleep environment:</strong> <strong>40dB or below</strong>

This means that achieving <strong>50-60dB sound isolation</strong> can transform "explosive thunder" into "distant thunder."

## Building Structure and Soundproofing Performance

Building structure directly affects sound isolation. Reference this table to identify your building type.

| Structure | Sound Isolation Performance (external wall) | Thunder Perception |
|---|---|---|
| Wooden construction (traditional) | 25-35dB | Resonates almost completely |
| Lightweight steel frame | 30-40dB | Explosive quality remains |
| RC construction (reinforced concrete) | 45-55dB | Mitigated to distant thunder level |
| RC construction + dual windows | 50-60dB | Safe for work-from-home and sleep |

<strong>Critical Point</strong>: Even in RC apartments, <strong>without window reinforcement, soundproofing effects are insufficient</strong>.

## Window Sound Isolation Enhancement: Top Priority

Thunder <strong>enters primarily through windows</strong>, making window treatment the highest priority.

### Dual Windows (Inner Sash)

<strong>Performance</strong>: +10-15dB improvement
<strong>Cost</strong>: ¥50,000-150,000 per window

The air gap between installed inner windows and existing sash dramatically improves sound isolation. Rental-friendly products allow restoration to original state.

### Soundproof Glass

<strong>Performance</strong>: +10-20dB improvement
<strong>Cost</strong>: ¥100,000-200,000 per window

Uses dual-pane or laminated glass. Requires existing glass replacement, making it suitable for owner-occupied homes.

### Soundproof Curtains

<strong>Performance</strong>: Several dB improvement (psychological reassurance effect)
<strong>Cost</strong>: ¥10,000-30,000 per window

While effects are limited, they reduce interior reflections and enhance psychological comfort.

### Storm Shutters & Soundproof Shutters

<strong>Standard Storm Shutters</strong>
- <strong>Performance:</strong> 5-6dB reduction
- <strong>Cost:</strong> Several thousand yen
- <strong>Features:</strong> Easy improvement

<strong>Soundproof Shutters</strong>
- <strong>Performance:</strong> Up to 30dB reduction
- <strong>Cost (manual):</strong> ¥60,000-160,000
- <strong>Cost (electric):</strong> ¥100,000-350,000
- <strong>Features:</strong> Blocks both sound and light, ideal for those sensitive to thunder

## Housing Selection Criteria

When house hunting, consider these selection priorities.

### Building Structure Priority Order

1. <strong>RC construction + dual windows</strong>: Ideal choice
2. <strong>RC construction alone</strong>: Window reinforcement sufficient
3. <strong>Lightweight steel frame</strong>: Multiple countermeasures necessary
4. <strong>Wooden house</strong>: Professional construction needed

### Location Selection Tips

<strong>Locations to Avoid</strong>
- <strong>Areas with frequent lightning (weather-map active zones)</strong>
- <strong>Hilltops where lightning strikes easily</strong>
- <strong>Near tall structures or transmission towers (lightning risk)</strong>

<strong>Locations to Choose</strong>
- <strong>Flat terrain</strong>
- <strong>Surrounded by buildings (urban canyon effect)</strong>
- <strong>Areas with minimal lightning damage history</strong>

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<strong>Q: Can soundproofing be done in rental housing?</strong>
A: Yes. Dual windows and soundproof curtains work in rentals. Consult management for construction work.

<strong>Q: What's most effective for wooden houses?</strong>
A: Prioritize window reinforcement (dual windows or shutters). With budget, soundproofing one bedroom is also effective.

<strong>Q: Is there an optimal season for thunder countermeasures?</strong>
A: Timing is flexible, but completing before rainy season (May-July) or autumn rain season (September-October) ensures peace of mind.

<strong>Q: Is building approval needed for soundproof room installation?</strong>
A: In apartments, management rules review is essential. Consult management if construction is required.
