The demand for shared streaming studios is rapidly increasing alongside the expansion of the video streaming market in Japan. Studios offering professional-quality streaming environments on an hourly basis are rapidly expanding, primarily in urban areas, serving everyone from individual creators to businesses.

This article analyzes the growth factors of the shared streaming studio market in Japan, pricing trends, utilization rate data by city, and market forecasts for 2025 and beyond, based on the latest data.

## Why is the Shared Streaming Studio Market Growing Rapidly?

The shared streaming studio market has maintained an annual growth rate of 15-20% since 2020. This rapid growth is driven by four main factors.

### Expansion of Video and Live Streaming Market

The overall expansion of the video streaming market is driving demand for shared studios. According to a survey by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) of Japan, the Japanese video streaming market reached approximately 2.5 trillion yen (approximately $17 billion USD) in 2024, recording growth of over 15% compared to the previous year.

Content creation demand on platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch is increasing, and the number of individual creators is rapidly growing. As of 2024, creators with over 10,000 YouTube channel subscribers in Japan reached approximately 150,000.

### Increase in Corporate Online Events and Webinar Demand

Since the COVID-19 pandemic, corporate online events and webinar demand have become established. As of 2024, approximately 75% of companies with 100 or more employees regularly hold online events.

Cases of companies using external studios for new product launches, corporate events, and online briefings are increasing. Particularly for corporate events requiring professional-quality streaming environments, shared studios have become mainstream.

### Increase in Individual Creators (YouTubers, VTubers, TikTok Creators)

The expansion of individual creator segments has become a major source of demand for shared studios.

<strong>YouTubers</strong>
- As of 2024, creators with over 100,000 YouTube channel subscribers in Japan number approximately 3,000
- Creators seeking high-quality filming environments are increasing

<strong>VTubers</strong>
- The VTuber market grew to approximately 20 billion yen (approximately $135 million USD) in 2024
- High-quality studio environments are essential for 3D model streaming

<strong>TikTok Creators</strong>
- The number of TikTok creators in the Japanese market reached approximately 5 million as of 2024
- Demand for filming in studios with "Instagram-worthy" backgrounds is rapidly increasing

### High Cost of Dedicated Studios → Rapid Growth in "Hourly Rental" Demand

The high construction and maintenance costs of dedicated studios are driving demand for shared studios.

Initial investment for dedicated studios, including soundproofing construction and equipment investment, costs 5 million to 20 million yen or more (approximately $34,000 to $135,000 USD). On the other hand, shared studios can be used for approximately 3,000 to 10,000 yen per hour (approximately $20 to $68 USD), making them a cost-effective option for individual creators and small-to-medium enterprises.

### Background of Shared Studios Concentrating in Urban Areas

Shared studios are rapidly expanding, primarily in urban areas. As of 2024, there are over 200 shared streaming studios within Tokyo's 23 wards.

Reasons for demand concentration in urban areas are as follows:

- <strong>Good Transportation Access</strong>: Easy access for creators and corporate staff
- <strong>Concentration of Equipment and Service Providers</strong>: Equipment vendors and technical staff needed for studio operations are concentrated
- <strong>Concentration of Demand</strong>: Creators and companies are concentrated in urban areas

## What are Shared Streaming Studios? | Features and Mechanisms

Shared streaming studios are streaming environment services with a "hourly rental × professional equipment" style. Users can utilize professional-quality streaming equipment for only the time they need.

### "Hourly Rental × Professional Equipment" Style

The basic model of shared studios is hourly rental. Users can rent studios from one hour to several hours and use professional-quality streaming equipment.

Typical usage times are 2-4 hours for individual creators and 4-8 hours for corporate events. Discount plans are available for long-term use or regular use.

### Soundproofing/Sound Insulation Design / High-Speed Internet / Lighting, Camera, Audio Equipment

Shared studios are equipped with everything needed for professional-quality streaming.

<strong>Soundproofing/Sound Insulation Design</strong>
- Sound insulation performance D-50 or higher (for general streaming purposes)
- D-60 or higher for premium studios
- Achieves both external sound blocking and internal sound leakage prevention

<strong>High-Speed Internet</strong>
- Upload speed of 100 Mbps or higher (4K streaming compatible)
- Wired LAN connection is standard
- Provides stable streaming environment

<strong>Lighting, Camera, Audio Equipment</strong>
- Professional LED lighting (3-point lighting set)
- 4K-compatible cameras (multiple units installed)
- High-quality microphones (condenser microphones, wireless microphones)
- Mixers and audio interfaces

### Professional Equipment such as Green Screens, White Cyc, Switchers

Premium studios are equipped with more advanced equipment.

<strong>Green Screens and White Cyc</strong>
- Green screens for chroma key compositing
- White cyc sets for white background filming
- Easy background changes

<strong>Switchers and Streaming Equipment</strong>
- Video switchers (multiple camera switching)
- Streaming encoders (OBS, vMix compatible)
- Real-time streaming environment

<strong>Other Professional Equipment</strong>
- Teleprompters (script display devices)
- Monitors (multiple units)
- Audio equipment (effects processors, compressors)

### Types of Operating Models: Hands-Free Use, Staff-Included, etc.

Shared studios have multiple operating models.

<strong>Basic Model (Self-Service)</strong>
- Users operate equipment themselves
- Relatively affordable pricing (3,000 to 8,000 yen per hour, approximately $20 to $54 USD)
- Equipment operation knowledge required

<strong>Hands-Free Use Model</strong>
- Studio side handles equipment setup
- Users can start streaming immediately upon arrival
- Pricing higher than basic model (5,000 to 12,000 yen per hour, approximately $34 to $81 USD)

<strong>Staff-Included Model</strong>
- Technical staff support streaming
- Camera work, audio adjustment, and streaming operations handled by staff
- High pricing (15,000 to 30,000 yen per hour, or 50,000 to 200,000 yen per slot, approximately $101 to $203 USD per hour, or $338 to $1,352 USD per slot)

## Pricing Trends | Differences Between Individuals and Businesses

Pricing for shared streaming studios differs significantly between individual and corporate users. Pricing also varies by location, equipment level, and usage time.

### Pricing Range for Individual Creators

Pricing for individual creators ranges from 3,000 to 18,000 yen per hour (approximately $20 to $122 USD).

<strong>Basic Pricing Range (3,000 to 10,000 yen per hour)</strong>
- Self-service type studios
- Basic streaming equipment (cameras, microphones, lighting)
- Outside city centers, or simplified studios in city centers

<strong>Premium City Center Range (10,000 to 18,000 yen per hour)</strong>
- City center areas such as Shibuya, Roppongi, and Shinjuku
- High-quality equipment (4K cameras, professional lighting, green screens)
- Hands-free use plans

<strong>Late-Night Pricing and Long-Term Rental Discounts</strong>
- 20-30% discount for late-night hours (10 PM to 6 AM)
- 10-20% discount for 4 hours or more usage
- Member discounts based on monthly usage frequency (10-15% discount)

### Pricing Range for Corporate/Event Studios

Pricing for corporate and event use is higher than for individuals.

<strong>Basic Pricing (15,000 to 30,000 yen per hour)</strong>
- Corporate-oriented studios
- High-quality equipment and spacious areas
- Multiple cameras and advanced audio equipment

<strong>Full-Set Plans (50,000 to 200,000 yen per slot)</strong>
- Includes technical staff and switching
- Streaming agency services
- Full event operation support

<strong>Additional Costs for Background Set Changes and Streaming Agency Services</strong>
- Background set changes: 5,000 to 15,000 yen (approximately $34 to $101 USD)
- Streaming agency (technical staff): 10,000 to 20,000 yen per hour (approximately $68 to $135 USD)
- Additional equipment rental: 5,000 to 20,000 yen per item (approximately $34 to $135 USD)

### Cost Structure (Reference)

Pricing for shared studios is based on the following cost structure.

<strong>Location Costs (Higher Near Stations)</strong>
- City center properties near stations: Monthly rent of 500,000 to 1,500,000 yen (approximately $3,380 to $10,140 USD)
- Regional cities: Monthly rent of 200,000 to 500,000 yen (approximately $1,352 to $3,380 USD)
- Location costs are reflected in pricing

<strong>Soundproofing Construction, Fiber Internet, Equipment Investment</strong>
- Soundproofing construction: 1 million to 5 million yen (approximately $6,760 to $33,800 USD, depending on scale)
- Fiber internet: 50,000 to 150,000 yen per month (approximately $338 to $1,014 USD, high-speed lines)
- Equipment investment: 2 million to 10 million yen (approximately $13,520 to $67,600 USD, depending on equipment level)

<strong>Securing Technical Staff</strong>
- Technical staff labor costs: 3,000 to 5,000 yen per hour (approximately $20 to $34 USD)
- Labor costs are reflected in pricing for staff-included plans
