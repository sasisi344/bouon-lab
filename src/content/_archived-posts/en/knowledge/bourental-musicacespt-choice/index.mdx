## What Are Soundproof Rentals? Key Differences from Instrument-Friendly Properties

### Soundproof Rental Definition

Soundproof rentals are <strong>rental properties equipped with dedicated soundproof rooms</strong>.

Rather than retrofitting regular apartments with soundproof rooms, these buildings incorporate soundproofing performance from the design stage. Essentially, "soundproofing" is standard equipment.

### 3 Major Differences from "Instrument-Friendly" Properties

Many people confuse "instrument-friendly properties" with "soundproof rentals." They're actually completely different.

<strong>1. Dramatically Different Sound Insulation</strong>

- <strong>Instrument-friendly:</strong> D-40~50 sound insulation (similar to regular apartments)
- <strong>Soundproof rental:</strong> D-50~80 sound insulation (with dedicated soundproof rooms)

These numbers mean: for every 10-point D-value increase, perceived volume roughly halves. So D-60 soundproof rentals have about <strong>one-quarter the sound leakage</strong> of D-40 instrument-friendly properties.

<strong>2. Completely Different Permissible Hours</strong>

- <strong>Instrument-friendly:</strong> Typically 9am-8pm restrictions, nighttime/early morning prohibited
- <strong>Soundproof rental:</strong> 24-hour practice OK (varies by property)

This is crucial for musicians. For those who can only practice after work at night or content creators streaming, 24-hour accessibility is invaluable.

<strong>3. Vastly Different Neighbor Trouble Risk</strong>

Even instrument-friendly properties commonly receive neighbor complaints. "Instrument-friendly" merely means "practice permitted," not "soundproof."

Conversely, soundproof rentals with dedicated soundproof rooms significantly minimize neighbor trouble risk when used properly.

## Types and Performance of Soundproof Rentals

### Type 1: Whole-Building Soundproofing

<strong>Characteristics:</strong>
- Entire building designed for soundproofing
- Every unit has soundproof features
- Community of music enthusiasts
- Premium pricing

<strong>Performance:</strong> D-60~80
<strong>Examples:</strong> Librante, Musision properties
<strong>Rent:</strong> 180,000-400,000 yen/month

### Type 2: Soundproof Room Unit in Standard Apartment

<strong>Characteristics:</strong>
- Regular apartment with soundproof booth
- 1.5-2 tatami soundproof room included
- Mix of regular and music-oriented residents
- More affordable

<strong>Performance:</strong> D-50~65
<strong>Rent:</strong> 120,000-250,000 yen/month

### Type 3: Reinforced Soundproofing (Music-Friendly Enhanced)

<strong>Characteristics:</strong>
- Enhanced soundproofing vs. regular apartments
- Not dedicated soundproof rooms
- Time restrictions usually apply
- Most affordable option

<strong>Performance:</strong> D-45~55
<strong>Rent:</strong> 100,000-180,000 yen/month
