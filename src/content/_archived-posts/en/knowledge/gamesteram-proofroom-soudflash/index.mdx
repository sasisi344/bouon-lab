Game streaming brings familiar challenges: "Keyboard and mouse clicking is so loud" or "Got complaints after screaming at horror games."

This article addresses game streaming-specific sound problems with practical solutions—from whole-room soundproofing to keyboard noise reduction.

## Organizing Game Streaming "Sound" Problems

First, let's categorize problematic sounds in game streaming.

### 3 Major Noise Sources

<strong>①Voice Sounds (Most Critical)</strong>
- Commentary
- Reactions
- Shouting (horror, competitive games)
- Singing (rhythm game streaming)

<strong>②Input Device Sounds (Surprisingly Loud)</strong>
- Keyboard typing
- Mouse clicking
- Controller buttons
- Mousepad friction

<strong>③Equipment Sounds (Easily Picked Up)</strong>
- PC fan noise
- Cooling fans
- Gaming chair squeaking
- Desk vibration

## Soundproofing Solutions by Noise Type

### Voice Soundproofing

<strong>Required D-Values:</strong>
- Normal commentary: D-45~50
- Excited reactions: D-50~55
- Shouting/screaming: D-55~65

<strong>Solutions by Budget:</strong>

<strong>Budget Tier (&lt;100,000 yen):</strong>
- Soundproof curtains: 10,000-30,000 yen
- Acoustic foam panels: 15,000-40,000 yen
- Door seals: 3,000-8,000 yen
- Expected: D-30~40 improvement

<strong>Mid-Range (100,000-500,000 yen):</strong>
- Vocal booth: 150,000-400,000 yen
- Comprehensive acoustic treatment: 50,000-150,000 yen
- Expected: D-40~50 performance

<strong>Premium (500,000+ yen):</strong>
- Unit-type soundproof room: 800,000-1,500,000 yen
- Expected: D-50~65 performance

### Keyboard/Mouse Noise Reduction

<strong>Silent Switches:</strong>
- Silent mechanical keyboards: 15,000-40,000 yen
- Silent mice: 5,000-15,000 yen
- Noise reduction: 50-70%

<strong>Desk Mats:</strong>
- Thick desk pad: 3,000-8,000 yen
- Reduces impact vibration
- Additional benefit: Comfortable typing

<strong>Microphone Technique:</strong>
- Directional mic positioning
- Away from keyboard/mouse
- Noise gate settings
- Software filtering

### Equipment Noise Management

<strong>PC Cooling:</strong>
- High-quality quiet fans: 3,000-8,000 yen each
- Liquid cooling: 15,000-50,000 yen
- Sound-dampening PC case: 10,000-30,000 yen

<strong>Chair Noise:</strong>
- Lubricate regularly
- Replace worn parts
- High-quality silent casters: 5,000-15,000 yen
- Proper weight distribution

## Horror Game Streaming Strategies

### Managing Inevitable Screaming

<strong>Soundproofing Approach:</strong>
- Minimum D-55 recommended
- Ideal: D-60~65
- Consider time restrictions even with soundproofing

<strong>Behavioral Adaptations:</strong>
- Stream horror games during daytime
- Warn neighbors in advance
- Practice volume control
- Use software limiters

<strong>Alternative Solutions:</strong>
- React intensely but quietly (facial expressions emphasized)
- Scheduled horror streams (daytime only)
- Pre-recorded reactions edited in post
- Accept genre limitations based on living situation

## Optimal Room Setup for Game Streaming

### Equipment Placement

<strong>Microphone Position:</strong>
- 20-30cm from mouth
- Angled away from keyboard
- Shock mount essential
- Pop filter recommended

<strong>Monitor Configuration:</strong>
- Reduces need to look down (better posture)
- Multi-monitor: Productivity boost
- Proper height: Eye level

<strong>Keyboard/Mouse:</strong>
- Ergonomic positioning
- Wrist support: Reduces impact force
- Proper desk height

### Acoustic Treatment

<strong>Priority Zones:</strong>
1. Behind/beside microphone: Highest priority
2. Walls mic faces: High priority
3. Ceiling above mic: Medium priority
4. Floor beneath: Useful for bass control

<strong>Budget Allocation:</strong>
- Essential panels: 20,000-40,000 yen
- Complete treatment: 50,000-100,000 yen
- Professional setup: 150,000-300,000 yen

## Software Solutions

### Noise Suppression

<strong>OBS Studio Filters:</strong>
- Noise suppression filter: Free
- Noise gate: Free
- Compressor: Free
- Effectiveness: 60-80% noise reduction

<strong>Dedicated Software:</strong>
- NVIDIA RTX Voice/Broadcast: Free (RTX GPU required)
- Krisp: ~$5/month
- Effectiveness: 85-95% noise reduction

<strong>AI Noise Cancellation:</strong>
- Latest advancement
- Real-time processing
- Excellent results
- Sometimes free, sometimes paid
