Game streaming brings familiar challenges: "Keyboard and mouse clicking is so loud" or "Got complaints after screaming at horror games."

This article addresses game streaming-specific sound problems with practical solutions—from whole-room soundproofing to keyboard noise reduction.

## Organizing Game Streaming "Sound" Problems

First, let's categorize problematic sounds in game streaming.

### 3 Major Noise Sources

<strong>①Voice Sounds (Most Critical)</strong>
- <strong>Commentary</strong>
- <strong>Reactions</strong>
- <strong>Shouting (horror, competitive games)</strong>
- <strong>Singing (rhythm game streaming)</strong>

<strong>②Input Device Sounds (Surprisingly Loud)</strong>
- <strong>Keyboard typing</strong>
- <strong>Mouse clicking</strong>
- <strong>Controller buttons</strong>
- <strong>Mousepad friction</strong>

<strong>③Equipment Sounds (Easily Picked Up)</strong>
- <strong>PC fan noise</strong>
- <strong>Cooling fans</strong>
- <strong>Gaming chair squeaking</strong>
- <strong>Desk vibration</strong>

## Soundproofing Solutions by Noise Type

### Voice Soundproofing

<strong>Required D-Values:</strong>
- <strong>Normal commentary:</strong> D-45~50
- <strong>Excited reactions:</strong> D-50~55
- <strong>Shouting/screaming:</strong> D-55~65

<strong>Solutions by Budget:</strong>

Budget Tier (&lt;100,000 yen):
- <strong>Soundproof curtains:</strong> 10,000-30,000 yen
- <strong>Acoustic foam panels:</strong> 15,000-40,000 yen
- <strong>Door seals:</strong> 3,000-8,000 yen
- <strong>Expected:</strong> D-30~40 improvement

<strong>Mid-Range (100,000-500,000 yen):</strong>
- <strong>Vocal booth:</strong> 150,000-400,000 yen
- <strong>Comprehensive acoustic treatment:</strong> 50,000-150,000 yen
- <strong>Expected:</strong> D-40~50 performance

<strong>Premium (500,000+ yen):</strong>
- <strong>Unit-type soundproof room:</strong> 800,000-1,500,000 yen
- <strong>Expected:</strong> D-50~65 performance

### Keyboard/Mouse Noise Reduction

<strong>Silent Switches:</strong>
- <strong>Silent mechanical keyboards:</strong> 15,000-40,000 yen
- <strong>Silent mice:</strong> 5,000-15,000 yen
- <strong>Noise reduction:</strong> 50-70%

<strong>Desk Mats:</strong>
- <strong>Thick desk pad:</strong> 3,000-8,000 yen
- <strong>Reduces impact vibration</strong>
- <strong>Additional benefit:</strong> Comfortable typing

<strong>Microphone Technique:</strong>
- <strong>Directional mic positioning</strong>
- <strong>Away from keyboard/mouse</strong>
- <strong>Noise gate settings</strong>
- <strong>Software filtering</strong>

### Equipment Noise Management

<strong>PC Cooling:</strong>
- <strong>High-quality quiet fans:</strong> 3,000-8,000 yen each
- <strong>Liquid cooling:</strong> 15,000-50,000 yen
- <strong>Sound-dampening PC case:</strong> 10,000-30,000 yen

<strong>Chair Noise:</strong>
- <strong>Lubricate regularly</strong>
- <strong>Replace worn parts</strong>
- <strong>High-quality silent casters:</strong> 5,000-15,000 yen
- <strong>Proper weight distribution</strong>

## Horror Game Streaming Strategies

### Managing Inevitable Screaming

<strong>Soundproofing Approach:</strong>
- <strong>Minimum D-55 recommended</strong>
- <strong>Ideal:</strong> D-60~65
- <strong>Consider time restrictions even with soundproofing</strong>

<strong>Behavioral Adaptations:</strong>
- <strong>Stream horror games during daytime</strong>
- <strong>Warn neighbors in advance</strong>
- <strong>Practice volume control</strong>
- <strong>Use software limiters</strong>

<strong>Alternative Solutions:</strong>
- <strong>React intensely but quietly (facial expressions emphasized)</strong>
- <strong>Scheduled horror streams (daytime only)</strong>
- <strong>Pre-recorded reactions edited in post</strong>
- <strong>Accept genre limitations based on living situation</strong>

## Optimal Room Setup for Game Streaming

### Equipment Placement

<strong>Microphone Position:</strong>
- <strong>20-30cm from mouth</strong>
- <strong>Angled away from keyboard</strong>
- <strong>Shock mount essential</strong>
- <strong>Pop filter recommended</strong>

<strong>Monitor Configuration:</strong>
- <strong>Reduces need to look down (better posture)</strong>
- <strong>Multi-monitor:</strong> Productivity boost
- <strong>Proper height:</strong> Eye level

<strong>Keyboard/Mouse:</strong>
- <strong>Ergonomic positioning</strong>
- <strong>Wrist support:</strong> Reduces impact force
- <strong>Proper desk height</strong>

### Acoustic Treatment

<strong>Priority Zones:</strong>
1. Behind/beside microphone: Highest priority
2. Walls mic faces: High priority
3. Ceiling above mic: Medium priority
4. Floor beneath: Useful for bass control

<strong>Budget Allocation:</strong>
- <strong>Essential panels:</strong> 20,000-40,000 yen
- <strong>Complete treatment:</strong> 50,000-100,000 yen
- <strong>Professional setup:</strong> 150,000-300,000 yen
