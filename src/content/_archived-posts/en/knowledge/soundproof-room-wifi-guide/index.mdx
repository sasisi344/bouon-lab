Struggling with weak Wi-Fi or slow internet speeds in your soundproof room? The structure of soundproof rooms in Japan (soundproofing materials, multi-layer walls, metal panels) naturally weakens Wi-Fi signals.

This article explains how to build a comfortable network environment in soundproof rooms. We'll introduce three solutions—router placement optimization, mesh Wi-Fi, and wired LAN—tailored for streamers, gamers, and remote workers.

## Conclusion: Wi-Fi Works in Soundproof Rooms, But Signal Attenuation Is Inevitable

You <strong>can</strong> use Wi-Fi in soundproof rooms. However, due to the structure, <strong>signal attenuation, speed reduction, and instability are difficult to avoid</strong>.

### Three Key Points About Wi-Fi in Soundproof Rooms

1. <strong>Usage Is Possible</strong>
- <strong>Wi-Fi signals do reach inside soundproof rooms</strong>
- <strong>Works normally when the door is open</strong>
- <strong>Light smartphone use often works fine</strong>

2. <strong>Speed Reduction and Instability Are Structurally Unavoidable</strong>
- <strong>Soundproofing materials, metal, and multi-layer structures block Wi-Fi signals</strong>
- <strong>Signal passes through "double walls" (outer room + soundproof room)</strong>
- <strong>Signal strength drops by -20 to -40dBm (about 50-90% attenuation)</strong>

3. <strong>Optimization Requires: Router Placement, Mesh Wi-Fi, or Wired LAN</strong>
- <strong>Move router closer to soundproof room (easiest method)</strong>
- <strong>Install mesh Wi-Fi relay point near soundproof room</strong>
- <strong>Install wired LAN for complete stability (for streaming/gaming)</strong>

<strong>Important: Optimal Solution Varies by Usage</strong>
- <strong>Light use (SNS, video watching):</strong> Router placement optimization sufficient
- <strong>Moderate use (remote work, online meetings):</strong> Mesh Wi-Fi recommended
- <strong>Heavy use (streaming, gaming, DTM):</strong> Wired LAN essential

## Why Communication Slows Down in Soundproof Rooms

Let's examine the structural reasons why Wi-Fi communication slows down in soundproof rooms.

### Reason ①: Soundproof Room Structure Weakens Radio Waves

Soundproof rooms are designed to "block sound," which simultaneously attenuates Wi-Fi signals.

<strong>Soundproof Room Structure and Wi-Fi Signal Relationship</strong>

|Structural Element|Wi-Fi Signal Impact|Attenuation|
|---|---|---|
| <strong>Soundproofing Material (Gypsum Board)</strong> |Absorbs radio waves|-5 to -10dBm|
| <strong>Metal Panels</strong> |Reflects/blocks radio waves|-10 to -20dBm|
| <strong>Multi-layer Structure (Walls/Ceiling/Floor)</strong> |Cumulative attenuation from multiple passes|-15 to -30dBm|
| <strong>Airtight Door</strong> |Limits radio wave entry points|-5 to -10dBm|

<strong>Double Walls: Outer Room + Soundproof Room</strong>
- <strong>Standard room wall:</strong> -3 to -5dBm
- <strong>Soundproof room wall:</strong> -15 to -30dBm
- <strong>Total:</strong> -18 to -35dBm attenuation (signal strength drops 60-99%)

<strong>Higher Soundproofing Performance = Weaker Radio Waves</strong>
- <strong>D-40 to 50 soundproof rooms:</strong> Wi-Fi relatively accessible
- <strong>D-55 to 65 soundproof rooms:</strong> Wi-Fi significantly attenuated
- <strong>D-70+ soundproof rooms:</strong> Wi-Fi may barely reach

### Reason ②: Distance and Obstacle Impact

Wi-Fi signal attenuation varies greatly depending on soundproof room placement.

<strong>Distance and Floor Impact</strong>

|Router and Soundproof Room Placement|Wi-Fi Signal Strength|Speed (Estimate)|
|---|---|---|
|Same room|★★★★★ (-30 to -40dBm)|50-100Mbps|
|Adjacent room|★★★★☆ (-50 to -60dBm)|20-50Mbps|
|1st floor router → 2nd floor room|★★☆☆☆ (-70 to -80dBm)|5-20Mbps|
|2nd floor router → 1st floor room|★★☆☆☆ (-70 to -80dBm)|5-20Mbps|

<strong>Different Floors Cause Severe Attenuation</strong>
- <strong>Floor/ceiling passage attenuation:</strong> -10 to -20dBm
- <strong>Soundproof room wall attenuation:</strong> -15 to -30dBm
- <strong>Total:</strong> -25 to -50dBm attenuation

<strong>Closing the Door Further Weakens Signal</strong>
- <strong>Soundproof doors are highly airtight with minimal radio wave entry points</strong>
- <strong>Door open:</strong> -20 to -30dBm attenuation
- <strong>Door closed:</strong> -30 to -40dBm attenuation
- <strong>Difference:</strong> about -10dBm (communication speed can drop to half or less)

### Reason ③: Internal Reflection and Airtightness Also Impact

Wi-Fi signal behavior inside soundproof rooms also affects communication quality.

<strong>Limited Entry Points Cause Instability</strong>
- <strong>Wi-Fi signals mainly enter through door gaps</strong>
- <strong>Signals reflect and interfere inside soundproof room</strong>
- <strong>Multipath (interference from multiple signal paths) occurs easily</strong>

<strong>Impact of Sound Absorbing Materials</strong>
- <strong>Sound absorbing materials (urethane foam, etc.) also absorb radio waves</strong>
- <strong>When entire walls have absorbing materials, radio wave reflection decreases</strong>
- <strong>Absorbing materials impact weak signals</strong>

## Common Communication Issues in Soundproof Rooms

Common issues when using Wi-Fi in soundproof rooms.

### Issue ①: Wi-Fi Antenna Shows 1-2 Bars

<strong>Symptoms</strong>
- <strong>Smartphone/PC Wi-Fi antenna shows 1-2 bars</strong>
- <strong>Signal strength drops below -70dBm</strong>
- <strong>Connection frequently drops</strong>

<strong>Causes</strong>
- <strong>Attenuation from soundproof room materials and metal structure</strong>
- <strong>Distance from router</strong>
- <strong>Using 5GHz band (weak against walls)</strong>

### Issue ②: Slow Video and Streaming Loading

<strong>Symptoms</strong>
- <strong>YouTube videos stop midway</strong>
- <strong>Streaming quality automatically drops to low quality</strong>
- <strong>Upload speed below 5Mbps</strong>

<strong>Causes</strong>
- <strong>Insufficient upload speed (streaming needs 10Mbps+)</strong>
- <strong>Unstable signal causes speed fluctuation</strong>
- <strong>Multiple connected devices</strong>

### Issue ③: Online Meeting Audio Delay

<strong>Symptoms</strong>
- <strong>Audio cuts out in Zoom/Teams/Google Meet</strong>
- <strong>Video stutters or freezes</strong>
- <strong>"Network is unstable" warning appears</strong>

<strong>Causes</strong>
- <strong>Packet loss from signal instability</strong>
- <strong>Latency (delay) over 100ms</strong>
- <strong>Upload speed below 3Mbps</strong>

### Issue ④: Game Lag Occurs

<strong>Symptoms</strong>
- <strong>FPS/fighting games/rhythm games stutter</strong>
- <strong>Ping value over 50ms (ideal is under 20ms)</strong>
- <strong>Connection drops during games</strong>

<strong>Causes</strong>
- <strong>Delay from Wi-Fi instability</strong>
- <strong>Packet loss over 1% (ideal is under 0.1%)</strong>
- <strong>Distance from router and obstacle impact</strong>

## Methods to Create Comfortable Network Environment in Soundproof Rooms

Three methods to use Wi-Fi comfortably in soundproof rooms.

### Method ①: Place Router Near Soundproof Room (Easiest)

The simplest and most effective method is <strong>moving the router closer to the soundproof room</strong>.

<strong>Router Placement Optimization</strong>

1. <strong>Simply Reducing Distance Improves Performance</strong>
- <strong>Ideal within 5m of soundproof room</strong>
- <strong>Same floor, preferably adjacent room</strong>
- <strong>Reduce to one wall's distance</strong>

2. <strong>Placing Router Near Door Is Effective</strong>
- <strong>Position router facing soundproof room door</strong>
- <strong>Signal enters easily through door gap</strong>
- <strong>Place on shelf or table (avoid floor placement)</strong>

<strong>Router Placement Examples</strong>

|Placement Location|Effect|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|Near soundproof room door (outside)|★★★★★|Most effective, signal enters directly|
|Same room as soundproof room|★★★★☆|Only one wall attenuation|
|Adjacent room (one wall)|★★★☆☆|Effect varies by wall material|
|Different floor|★☆☆☆☆|Floor/ceiling attenuation reduces effect|

<strong>Router Placement Notes</strong>
- <strong>Point router antenna toward soundproof room</strong>
- <strong>Avoid metal shelves/furniture (reflects radio waves)</strong>
- <strong>Adjust router height to 1-2m from floor</strong>

<strong>Effect Estimate</strong>
- <strong>Communication speed may improve 2-3x</strong>
- <strong>Budget:</strong> ¥0 (approx. $0) - just moving existing router
- <strong>Time required:</strong> 10-30 minutes

### Method ②: Use Mesh Wi-Fi (Stable Without Construction)

If moving the router isn't enough, <strong>mesh Wi-Fi</strong> is effective.

<strong>Mesh Wi-Fi Features</strong>

- <strong>Add satellite (child unit) near soundproof room</strong>
- <strong>Higher stability than repeaters with multiple points</strong>
- <strong>Improves home-wide network quality</strong>
- <strong>Seamless switching (connection doesn't drop)</strong>

<strong>Mesh Wi-Fi Installation Method</strong>

1. <strong>Place Main Unit Near Modem</strong>
- <strong>Replace existing router with mesh Wi-Fi main unit</strong>
- <strong>Or connect to existing router (bridge mode)</strong>

2. <strong>Place Satellite Outside Soundproof Room (Near Door)</strong>
- <strong>Position within 3-5m of soundproof room door</strong>
- <strong>Just plug into outlet to complete installation</strong>
- <strong>Signal enters soundproof room easily</strong>

3. <strong>Cover Entire Home with Multiple Satellites</strong>
- <strong>2-story homes/3LDK+ recommend 2-3 satellites</strong>
- <strong>Other rooms become comfortable too</strong>

<strong>Recommended Mesh Wi-Fi Products</strong>

|---|---|---|---|
|Google Nest Wifi|¥18,000~ ($120~)|Up to 200m² (2 units)|Easy setup, app management|
|TP-Link Deco X20|¥15,000~ ($100~)|Up to 260m² (2 units)|Good value, Wi-Fi 6 support|
|ASUS ZenWiFi AX|¥30,000~ ($200~)|Up to 500m² (2 units)|High performance, for gamers|

<strong>Mesh Wi-Fi Effect</strong>
- <strong>Communication speed improves 3-5x</strong>
- <strong>Signal strength improves to -50 to -60dBm (3-4 antenna bars)</strong>
- <strong>Budget:</strong> ¥15,000-30,000 ($100-200)
- <strong>Installation possible without construction</strong>

### Method ③: Install Wired LAN (Highest Stability)

The <strong>most stable</strong> method for streaming, gaming, and DTM is installing wired LAN.

<strong>Wired LAN Benefits</strong>

1. <strong>Nearly Zero Latency</strong>
- <strong>Ping value 5-20ms (1/3 to 1/5 of Wi-Fi)</strong>
- <strong>Strongest for streaming/gaming/DTM</strong>
- <strong>Packet loss rarely occurs</strong>

2. <strong>Speed Always at Maximum</strong>
- <strong>No distance or obstacle impact like Wi-Fi</strong>
- <strong>Consistently achieves 90-95% of theoretical speed</strong>
- <strong>1Gbps LAN achieves 900-950Mbps actual speed</strong>

3. <strong>No Noise or Surrounding Radio Wave Interference</strong>
- <strong>Not affected by other devices like Wi-Fi</strong>
- <strong>Extremely high stability</strong>

4. <strong>Easy to Branch to Multiple Indoor Devices</strong>
- <strong>Distribute to PC/audio interface/etc. with switching hub</strong>
- <strong>Complete wired LAN environment indoors</strong>

<strong>Wired LAN Installation Method</strong>

1. <strong>Place Router Outside Soundproof Room</strong>
- <strong>No need to place inside</strong>
- <strong>Run LAN cable from outside</strong>

2. <strong>Plan LAN Cable "Route"</strong>
- <strong>Under door gap (easiest method)</strong>
- <strong>Dedicated cable hole (secure during soundproof room design)</strong>
- <strong>Run together through power outlet</strong>

3. <strong>Connect to Switching Hub Inside Room</strong>
- <strong>Install 5-8 port switching hub</strong>
- <strong>Distribute to PC/audio interface/game console/etc.</strong>

<strong>Wired LAN Installation Notes</strong>
- <strong>Unit-type soundproof rooms (Yamaha/Kawai/etc.) often prohibit drilling</strong>
- <strong>Always confirm in advance for rentals</strong>
- <strong>Secure LAN hole during DIY soundproof room planning</strong>

<strong>Wired LAN Effect</strong>
- <strong>Maximum communication speed (100Mbps-1Gbps)</strong>
- <strong>Minimum latency (Ping 5-20ms)</strong>
- <strong>Budget:</strong> ¥2,000-10,000 ($15-70) - LAN cable and hub
- <strong>Essential level for streaming/gaming</strong>

## Notes for Wired LAN Installation

Detailed notes for installing wired LAN in soundproof rooms.

### Note ①: Unit-Type Soundproof Rooms May Prohibit Drilling

<strong>Note for Yamaha/Kawai and Other Manufacturer Products</strong>
- <strong>Manufacturer warranty may become void</strong>
- <strong>Risk of soundproofing performance decline</strong>
- <strong>Always confirm in advance for rentals</strong>

<strong>Solutions When Drilling Is Prohibited</strong>
- <strong>Use under-door gap (if 1-2cm gap exists)</strong>
- <strong>Utilize ventilation port (run LAN cable through existing vent)</strong>
- <strong>Use Wi-Fi repeater/mesh Wi-Fi</strong>

### Note ②: Secure LAN Hole During DIY Soundproof Room Planning

<strong>Design Power/LAN/Ventilation Together</strong>
- <strong>Combine power outlet and LAN outlet in same location</strong>
- <strong>Share with ventilation port (secure cable space)</strong>
- <strong>Decide position during design stage</strong>

<strong>Drilling Existing Structure Risks Soundproofing Decline</strong>
- <strong>One hole can reduce D-value by 5-10</strong>
- <strong>Lost airtightness causes sound leakage</strong>
- <strong>Consult specialists for proper reinforcement</strong>

### Note ③: Reinforcement When Drilling Is Necessary

<strong>Seal Surroundings with Soundproofing Mats/Sheets</strong>
- <strong>Apply soundproofing sheet double-layer around hole perimeter</strong>
- <strong>Fill gap between cable and hole with soundproofing mat</strong>
- <strong>Apply vibration-damping mat around area for vibration control</strong>

<strong>Double Seal with Airtight Tape</strong>
- <strong>Apply airtight tape to hole edges</strong>
- <strong>Seal gap with airtight tape after running cable</strong>
- <strong>Complete sealing is difficult but minimizes actual harm</strong>

<strong>Accept That Original Performance Won't Fully Recover</strong>
- <strong>D-50 may decline to D-45~48</strong>
- <strong>Complete restoration difficult but may stay within acceptable range</strong>
- <strong>Consider professional construction if necessary</strong>

## Additional Techniques for Wireless Environment Improvement

Additional techniques to further optimize Wi-Fi environment.

### Technique ①: Switch Frequency Bands

Wi-Fi has two frequency bands with different characteristics.

<strong>2.4GHz vs 5GHz Comparison</strong>

|Frequency Band|Speed|Obstacle Strength|Soundproof Room Recommendation|
|---|---|---|---|
| <strong>2.4GHz</strong> |Max 600Mbps|Strong (passes through walls)|★★★★☆ Recommended|
| <strong>5GHz</strong> |Max 1300Mbps|Weak (weak against walls)|★★☆☆☆ Not recommended|

<strong>2.4GHz Is Stable in Soundproof Rooms</strong>
- <strong>5GHz:</strong> Fast but attenuates easily (-10 to -15dBm more attenuation)
- <strong>2.4GHz:</strong> Strong against obstacles, stable (suitable for soundproof rooms)
- <strong>Can fix frequency band in router settings</strong>

### Technique ②: Device-Side Optimization

<strong>Wi-Fi 6 / Wi-Fi 6E Support Increases Stability</strong>
- <strong>Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax):</strong> Strong against congestion, multiple device support
- <strong>Wi-Fi 6E:</strong> 6GHz band support (legalized in Japan in 2022)
- <strong>PC/smartphone antenna sensitivity also impacts</strong>

<strong>Device-Side Wi-Fi Settings</strong>
- <strong>Update Wi-Fi adapter driver to latest</strong>
- <strong>Turn off power saving mode (Wi-Fi adapter power management)</strong>
- <strong>Optimize roaming settings (when using mesh Wi-Fi)</strong>

### Technique ③: Router Selection

<strong>Beamforming Models Suit Soundproof Rooms</strong>
- <strong>Technology to concentrate radio waves in specific direction</strong>
- <strong>Can strengthen radio waves toward soundproof room</strong>
- <strong>Installed in Wi-Fi 5 (ac) and later routers</strong>

<strong>Old Generation Routers Cause Speed Decline</strong>
- <strong>Wi-Fi 4 (n) and earlier routers recommend replacement</strong>
- <strong>Choose Wi-Fi 5 (ac) or later routers</strong>
- <strong>Wi-Fi 6 (ax) compatible routers ideal</strong>
