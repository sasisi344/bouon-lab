## 1. How does a "Soundproof Room" work in the first place?

The fact that sound disappears when you enter a soundproof room is not magic.
It consists of three scientific mechanisms: "<strong>Sound Insulation (the power to bounce back)</strong>", "<strong>Sound Absorption (the power to absorb)</strong>", and "<strong>Floating Structure (the power to cut off vibration)</strong>".

Specifically, knowing "Why is it heavy?" and "Why is it floating from the floor?" will help you understand the greatness of soundproof rooms.

---

## 2. What types are there? (3 types)

Even if you say "soundproof room" in one word, there are various types, from those you can buy for $1,000 to full-scale ones that cost $30,000 or more.
There are three main types: "<strong>Simple Assembly</strong>", "<strong>Unit Type</strong>", and "<strong>Soundproof Construction</strong>", and the correct answer for you depends on your application (what kind of instrument you play).

---

## 3. How quiet does it get? (Effect and Performance)

The numbers "Dr-30" or "Dr-40" in the catalog. Can you imagine specifically how quiet these are?
We will explain at the experience level "How it sounds in the next room" for each type of sound, such as "human voice", "piano", and "drums".

## 4. Will I regret buying it? (Merits and Demerits)

Behind the best advantage of "Being able to play instruments anytime!", there are realistic realities such as "<strong>The room gets smaller</strong>", "<strong>It's hot in summer</strong>", and "<strong>Moving is difficult</strong>".
