"The room is narrow, but I want a quiet environment"
"I want to create my own booth for video streaming or telework"

The choice most selected by such people now is the <strong>compact soundproof room of 0.8 to 1 tatami size</strong>.
This size used to be called "narrow and hard to use", but with the evolution of technology and changes in needs, it has become a very practical choice.

In this article, we will provide guidelines for making the most of the minimum size soundproof room.

---

## "What You Can and Can't Do" in a 0.8 to 1 Tatami Soundproof Room

First, let's grasp the reality of this sense of size.

### What You Can Do
*   <strong>Video Streaming/Game Commentating</strong>: This is perfectly possible with a desk setup including a microphone and one monitor.
*   <strong>Telework/Web Conferences</strong>: You get a concentrated space where no one bothers you.
*   <strong>Vocal Recording</strong>: There's plenty of room in 1 tatami if you're just standing and singing.
*   <strong>Violin/Flute Performance</strong>: It's possible to perform while sitting or by adjusting your posture.

### What You Can't Do
*   <strong>Install an Upright Piano</strong>: It does not fit physically.
*   <strong>Use with Multiple People</strong>: It is for single person use only.
*   <strong>Big Actions</strong>: It is difficult to swing a guitar or move around vigorously.

---

## Two Choices for the Minimum Size: Simple Type vs Unit Type

When considering the 0.8 tatami class, there are the following two routes.

### 1. Simple Soundproof Room (Danbotchi, OTODASU, etc.)
*   <strong>Price</strong>: Around $600 to $2,000.
*   <strong>Merit</strong>: Cheap and light. Easy to assemble and disassemble yourself.
*   <strong>Who it's for</strong>: Streamers who want to "reduce voice leakage to the next room a little" or "suppress reverberation".

### 2. Unit Soundproof Room (Yamaha Avitecs, Kawai Nasal, etc.)
*   <strong>Price</strong>: Around $8,000 to $12,000.
*   <strong>Merit</strong>: Authentic sound insulation performance (Dr-35 to 40). Many models allow for air conditioning installation.
*   <strong>Who it's for</strong>: Those who want to "sing in the middle of the night in an apartment" or "perfectly shut out outside noise".

---

## Use Narrow Space Widely! Layout Tips

In a limited space of 1 tatami, a 1cm placement error leads to a feeling of pressure.

1.  <strong>Desks should be "Width" rather than "Depth"</strong>
    If there is too much depth, there will be no room to pull back the chair. It's a rule to choose a desk with a depth of 45 to 50 cm and float the space with a monitor arm.
2.  <strong>Effective Use of Walls</strong>
    Don't put things on the floor; use wall-mounted storage that also serves as acoustic panels.
3.  <strong>Lighting and Color Use</strong>
    By choosing bright lighting and an interior (or acoustic material color) based on white, you can alleviate visual claustrophobia.

---

## Summary: 0.8 Tatami/1 Tatami will be the "Best Work Desk"

If you throw away the stereotype that "soundproof room = musical instrument", this minimum size becomes the <strong>strongest concentration booth</strong> in modern times.

Especially for those living in a studio apartment of around 6 tatami, the benefit of being able to physically separate the living space from the working space is immeasurable.
