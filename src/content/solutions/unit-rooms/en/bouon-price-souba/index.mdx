## 🚀 The Short Answer
- <strong>DIY/Low-Budget (`$100 - $500`)</strong>: Acoustic panels and door weatherstripping. Effective for reducing echo (reverb), but extremely poor at blocking noise to/from neighbors.
- <strong>Japanese Prefab Booths (`$4,000 - $15,000`)</strong>: "Unit Soundproof Rooms" like Yamaha or Kawai. Achieving -35dB to -40dB (STC 40-45 equivalent) without any permanent construction. 
- <strong>Professional Construction (`$15,000+`)</strong>: Room-in-Room (Floating) structures. The ultimate solution, but generally not possible in rental properties.
- <strong>Soundproof Apartments (`$1,000~/month`)</strong>: Specialized rental properties in Japan (e.g., Musision). A unique system where you get a 24/7 playing environment for just your monthly rent.
