## How to Choose and Purchase Soundproof Sheets

### What Are Soundproof Sheets

Soundproof sheets are materials for blocking sound.

<strong>Features</strong>

- <strong>Heavy, high-density materials</strong>
- <strong>Significantly reduce sound transmission</strong>
- <strong>Used on walls and floors</strong>

<strong>Price Range</strong>: ¥50,000-100,000 (for approximately 6-tatami)

### How to Choose Soundproof Sheets

<strong>Selection Points</strong>

1. <strong>Thickness</strong>: Thicker = higher soundproof performance (1mm-3mm)
2. <strong>Density</strong>: Higher density = higher soundproof performance
3. <strong>Material</strong>: Lead sheet, rubber type, resin type, etc.

<strong>Recommended Products</strong>

- <strong>Lead Sheet:</strong> Highest soundproof performance but expensive
- <strong>Rubber Soundproof Sheet:</strong> Good balance
- <strong>Resin Soundproof Sheet:</strong> Lightweight and easy to handle

## How to Choose and Purchase Sound Absorption Materials (Glass Wool)

### What Are Sound Absorption Materials

Sound absorption materials are materials for absorbing sound and reducing reverberation.

<strong>Features</strong>

- <strong>Porous materials</strong>
- <strong>Convert sound energy to heat</strong>
- <strong>Reduce indoor reverberation</strong>

<strong>Price Range</strong>: ¥50,000-100,000 (for approximately 6-tatami)

### How to Choose Sound Absorption Materials

<strong>Selection Points</strong>

1. <strong>Density</strong>: Higher density = higher sound absorption performance
2. <strong>Thickness</strong>: Thicker = better low-frequency absorption (50mm-100mm)
3. <strong>Material</strong>: Glass wool, rock wool, urethane, etc.

<strong>Recommended Products</strong>

- <strong>Glass Wool:</strong> Most common and affordable
- <strong>Rock Wool:</strong> Higher performance than glass wool but expensive
- <strong>Urethane Sound Absorption Material:</strong> Lightweight and easy to handle
