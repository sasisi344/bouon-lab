## The Engineer's 3-Point Viewing Checklist

Tokyo's market moves fast. Use these physical checks during your 10-minute viewing:

1.  <strong>Duct Mufflers</strong>:
    Turn on the AC. Do you hear street noise coming through the vent? Poorly treated ventilation is the #1 "acoustic leak" in Tokyo apartments.
2.  <strong>Cremone Handles</strong>:
    Check if windows use airtight locking mechanisms (Cremone handles). Without a proper seal, Tokyo's traffic hum will ruin your sensitive recordings.
3.  <strong>Floor Load Limits</strong>:
    If you plan to install an additional booth (e.g., 1.5-tatami size), confirm the floor is reinforced for 180kg/m²+.
