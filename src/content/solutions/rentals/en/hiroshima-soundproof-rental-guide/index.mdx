"I'm looking for a room where I can play instruments in Hiroshima, but I don't know where to live..."

If you are doing musical activities in Hiroshima, the answer might simply be: <strong>"Naka-ku"</strong>.
Hiroshima has a face as a "City of Music", home to a professional orchestra (Hiroshima Symphony) and a historic music university in the city center.

In this article, we explain how to efficiently find soundproof/instrument-allowed properties focusing on Hiroshima's unique "Music College Area".

## Conclusion: Rentals concentrate in "Naka-ku (Around Music Univ.)"

The biggest key point when searching in Hiroshima City is the existence of <strong>"Elisabeth University of Music"</strong>.

Located in Noboricho, Naka-ku, many students and lecturers live around this university. Therefore, real estate agencies <strong>concentrate "Instrument Negotiable" properties in this specific area.</strong>
It is overwhelmingly more efficient to narrow your search to "Around the University" rather than searching blindly on general sites.

### Rent Market: ¥60,000+ for 1R/1K

Since Naka-ku is near the city center (Hatchobori/Kamiya-cho), rent is slightly higher than other wards.

- <strong>Soundproof Mansion (1R/1K)</strong>: <strong>¥65,000 - ¥80,000 (approx. $430 - $530 USD)</strong>
- <strong>Student Apartment (Instrument Negotiable)</strong>: <strong>¥50,000s (approx. $330 USD)</strong>

## Target Areas and Features

### 1. Naka-ku (Noboricho / Kanayama-cho Area)

Within walking distance to Elisabeth Univ. and close to Hiroshima Station via streetcar.

- <strong>Features</strong>: Highest number of properties. Options range from 24-hour soundproof mansions to "Instrument Negotiable" apartments with playing time limits.
- <strong>Note</strong>: Some properties are close to the entertainment district (Nagarekawa), so strictly check the nighttime environment during viewing.

### 2. Minami-ku / Higashi-ku (Hiroshima Station Area)

Just across the river from Naka-ku.

- <strong>Features</strong>: Rent tends to be cheaper than Naka-ku. If you can commute by bicycle, you might find good rooms in the ¥50,000 range.

### 3. Search Tips

In Hiroshima, the mainstream style is <strong>"Instrument Negotiable in Standard Properties (Piano OK)"</strong> rather than "Fully Soundproof Rooms".

- <strong>Must Check</strong>: Rules are specific (e.g., "Upright Piano OK, Grand Piano NG", "No Brass Instruments"). Always confirm your instrument type with the agent.

## Music Studio Situation in Hiroshima

If playing at home is difficult, or you want to practice late at night, use external studios. Hiroshima has a strong band culture and good studios.

- <strong>Studio Jam (Asaminami-ku, etc.)</strong>: Popular among local bands. Good gear.
- <strong>NORI SOUND (Naka-ku)</strong>: Has 24-hour studios, perfect for late-night practice.
- <strong>Ward Cultural Centers (Kumin Bunka Center)</strong>: Each ward in Hiroshima City has cultural centers with "Music Rooms" or "Practice Rooms" available at very low rates. Great for students wanting to save money.

## Recommended Rent Search Sites & Keywords

Apart from major national sites, try these local search methods.

- <strong>Kanaderoom (Hiroshima Version)</strong>: Check market prices here first.
- <strong>Student Rental Sites (UniLife, Able Student Support, etc.)</strong>:
  - <strong>Hidden Gems</strong>: Even if labeled "Student Only", they often introduce similar "Instrument Negotiable" properties owned by the same landlord to non-students. Check special features for "Elisabeth Univ. Area".

## Summary: Move with the Music Students

The instrument rental market in Hiroshima moves in sync with music students (Enrollment/Graduation).
The peak season for new listings is <strong>"January to March"</strong>.
Good rooms fill up immediately after university acceptance results are announced.

If you want to live with music in Hiroshima, target "Naka-ku" and move quickly during the spring season.
