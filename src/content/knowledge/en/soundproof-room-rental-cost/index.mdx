"I can't afford $10,000, but I want a soundproof room right now."
"I don't know if it will last, so I want to try it out for now."

<strong>Soundproof room rental (subscription) services</strong> are popular with such people.
The most famous is Yamaha's "Oto-Rent". It is an attractive service where Avitecs arrives at your home from the $100 range per month, but <strong>if you rent without thinking about anything, it might end up being \"it was cheaper to buy\".</strong>

In this article, we will explain the mechanism and realistic costs of soundproof room rental, and the judgment line of "should I buy or rent?".

## Mechanism and Cost of Soundproof Room Rental (In the case of Yamaha Oto-Rent)

Basically, you can install a "new" or "used" soundproof room at home with a monthly payment.

### 1. Market Price of Monthly fee

- <strong>0.8 Tatami Type:</strong> $120 to $140 per month
- <strong>1.2 Tatami Type:</strong> $150 to $170 per month
- <strong>1.5 Tatami Type:</strong> $170 to $200 per month
- <strong>2.0 Tatami Type:</strong> $220 to $250 per month

It seems like "a soundproof room for a little over $100 a month!", but that's not all.

### 2. "Initial Costs" that are Charged Heavily at the Beginning

This is a trap.
At the start of the rental, the following costs must be paid in full.

- <strong>Registration Fee:</strong> around $20
- <strong>First Monthly fee:</strong> 1 month's worth of rental fee
- <strong>Transportation and Assembly Cost:</strong> <strong>This is expensive! (about $400 to $1,000)</strong>
- <strong>Air Conditioner Construction Fee:</strong> If necessary (about $300 to $500)

In other words, <strong>cash of "about $1,000 to $1,500" is required at the moment of renting</strong>. It doesn't end with only an "administrative fee of $30" like a smartphone contract.

## "Rental" vs "Purchase" Which is a Better Deal?

The biggest feature of rental is that there is a system where "you can apply the paid rental fee and buy it as it is" (in the case of Oto-Rent).

However, if you rent for too long, the total purchase amount will be higher than the fixed price due to interest and fees.

### The Break-Even Point is "15 to 24 Months"

It's just an estimate, but you won't lose if you think by the following criteria.

- <strong>Use within 1 year (12 months):</strong> <strong>[Rental is a deal]</strong>
- <strong>"I want to use it only for 1 year of the exam" or "I might move soon due to job transfer".</strong>
- <strong>The total amount is cheaper than buying, and there is no trouble of disposal.</strong>
- <strong>Plan to use for 2 years or more:</strong> <strong>[Purchase by loan is a deal]</strong>
- <strong>If you keep paying for 2 years or more, the total payment amount often exceeds the list price.</strong>
- <strong>If you use it for a long time, buying with a shopping loan (such as 60 payments) from the beginning may make the monthly payment amount cheaper.</strong>

## People for whom Rental is Suitable / Not Suitable

### Recommended for

- <strong>People who want to test if "life with a soundproof room" works:</strong> Ideal for trying out for a few months if you can withstand the pressure and really practice.
- <strong>People who are temporary bachelors for a limited time:</strong> When it is decided to surely let go within 2 years.
- <strong>Students (Examinees):</strong> For a limited time until the music college entrance exam, etc.

### Not Recommended for

- <strong>People who rent it "for the time being" and seem to use it for about 3 years for some reason:</strong> You will lose the most.
- <strong>People who are difficult to prepare initial costs ($1,000 -):</strong> If you pass the loan screening, buying by loan may cost zero for initial costs.

## Summary

Soundproof room rental is the strongest service for "trials" and "limited time".
However, it has a nature of being expensive in the long run.

Please choose whether to "own (purchase)" or "use (rental)" according to your own life plan.
