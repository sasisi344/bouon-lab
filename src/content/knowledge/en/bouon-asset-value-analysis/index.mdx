### Depreciation Logic
For business users, a soundproof room is typically categorized as <strong>"Furniture & Fixtures"</strong> with a tax life of <strong>8–15 years.</strong> Because the actual market value drops much slower than the accounting book value, you often hold "hidden equity" in the unit.

## 3. Top 3 Rules to Maximize Resale Value

Protecting your asset value starts on day one.

1.  <strong>Stick to Major Brands</strong>: Yamaha and Kawai have established buyback networks, ensuring instant liquidity.
2.  <strong>No-Drill AC Installation</strong>: Use [dedicated mounting plates](proofroom-aircondition-select) instead of drilling into the booth panels. This saves hundreds in appraisal deductions.
3.  <strong>Environment Control</strong>: Non-smoking and pet-free environments are mandatory. Odors can slash resale value by <strong>20-30%</strong> instantly.
