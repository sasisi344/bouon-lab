## After Each Use Cleaning (10 Minutes)

Three tasks to perform after using your soundproof room.

### Task 1: Ventilation (10 Minutes)

<strong>Why Ventilation is Most Important</strong>

- <strong>Removes moisture to prevent mold</strong>
- <strong>Reduces CO2 levels for comfort</strong>
- <strong>Protects instruments from humidity</strong>
- <strong>Prevents odors</strong>

<strong>Effective Ventilation Steps</strong>

1. Open door fully immediately after use
2. Turn on ventilation fan (if available)
3. Use air circulator for better circulation (recommended)
4. Leave for 10 minutes
5. Confirm humidity has dropped before closing

<strong>Air Circulator Tips</strong>

|Item|Details|
|---|---|
| <strong>Placement</strong> |Direct airflow outward from room|
| <strong>Angle</strong> |From floor toward ceiling for circulation|
| <strong>Cost</strong> |¥5,000-15,000 ($35-100)|
| <strong>Effect</strong> |Doubles ventilation efficiency|

<strong>What Happens Without Ventilation?</strong>

- <strong>1 week:</strong> Moisture begins accumulating
- <strong>1 month:</strong> Mold spores develop
- <strong>3 months:</strong> Visible mold appears
- <strong>1 year:</strong> Sound insulation materials begin deteriorating

<strong>10 minutes of ventilation after each use is the strongest mold prevention measure.</strong>

### Task 2: Dry Wiping (3 Minutes)

<strong>Areas to Wipe</strong>

- <strong>Entire floor (especially where sweat dropped)</strong>
- <strong>Wall areas touched by hands</strong>
- <strong>Instruments (remove sweat and fingerprints)</strong>
- <strong>Door handle area</strong>

<strong>Tools Needed</strong>

- <strong>Microfiber cloth (¥500-1,000 / $3-7)</strong>
- <strong>Existing towels work too</strong>
- <strong>Use dry only</strong>

<strong>Dry Wiping Tips</strong>

1. <strong>No Water</strong>
- <strong>Wet wiping only once weekly</strong>
- <strong>Daily wiping should be dry only</strong>
- <strong>Moisture causes mold</strong>

2. <strong>Wipe Gently</strong>
- <strong>Don't scrub hard</strong>
- <strong>Be especially careful with sound-absorbing materials</strong>
- <strong>Avoid damage</strong>

3. <strong>Wash Dirty Cloths</strong>
- <strong>Wash weekly</strong>
- <strong>Use clean cloths</strong>
- <strong>Having multiple cloths is convenient</strong>

### Task 3: Debris Removal (2 Minutes)

<strong>Areas to Check</strong>

- <strong>Floor corners</strong>
- <strong>Under instruments</strong>
- <strong>Below ventilation fan</strong>
- <strong>Door gaps</strong>

<strong>Items to Remove</strong>

- <strong>Dust</strong>
- <strong>Hair</strong>
- <strong>Paper scraps</strong>
- <strong>Instrument fragments (picks, etc.)</strong>

<strong>Debris Removal Tips</strong>

- <strong>Visual inspection</strong>
- <strong>Hand removal is sufficient</strong>
- <strong>Vacuum cleaning once weekly is enough</strong>
- <strong>Don't miss small debris</strong>

## Weekly Cleaning (30 Minutes)

Once weekly, perform thorough cleaning.

### 5-Step Weekly Cleaning

|Step|Task|Time|
|---|---|---|
| <strong>1. Preparation</strong> |Remove instruments and equipment|5 min|
| <strong>2. Vacuuming</strong> |Clean entire floor|10 min|
| <strong>3. Floor Mopping</strong> |Wipe with well-wrung cloth|10 min|
| <strong>4. Wall Wiping</strong> |Dry wipe or well-wrung cloth|5 min|
| <strong>5. Inspection</strong> |Check filters and door seals|5 min|

### Step 1: Preparation (5 Minutes)

<strong>Why Remove Instruments and Equipment</strong>

- <strong>Enables thorough cleaning in corners</strong>
- <strong>Prevents detergent contact with instruments</strong>
- <strong>Improves work efficiency</strong>

<strong>Items to Remove</strong>

- <strong>Instruments (guitar, keyboard, etc.)</strong>
- <strong>Microphone stands</strong>
- <strong>Chairs</strong>
- <strong>Small items</strong>

### Step 2: Vacuuming (10 Minutes)

<strong>Vacuuming Points</strong>

1. <strong>Clean from corners toward center</strong>
- <strong>Dust accumulates in corners</strong>
- <strong>Gather toward center and vacuum</strong>

2. <strong>Thoroughly clean below ventilation fan</strong>
- <strong>Dust accumulates here</strong>
- <strong>Clean weekly without fail</strong>

3. <strong>Don't forget wall edges</strong>
- <strong>Boundary between walls and floor</strong>
- <strong>Dust accumulates easily</strong>

4. <strong>Be gentle with sound-absorbing materials</strong>
- <strong>Don't use strong suction</strong>
- <strong>Avoid damage</strong>

<strong>Vacuum Cleaner Selection</strong>

- <strong>Existing vacuum is fine</strong>
- <strong>Handheld types are convenient</strong>
- <strong>Cordless makes work easier</strong>
- <strong>Normal suction power is sufficient</strong>

### Step 3: Floor Mopping (10 Minutes)

<strong>Proper Mopping Procedure</strong>

1. Fill bucket with water
2. Add small amount of neutral detergent (few drops)
3. Soak cloth and wring tightly (important)
4. Wipe entire floor
5. Ventilate for 10 minutes to dry

<strong>Wet Mopping Precautions</strong>

|Precaution|Reason|
|---|---|
| <strong>Wring cloth tightly</strong> |Remaining water causes mold|
| <strong>Use little detergent</strong> |Excess damages flooring|
| <strong>Dry after wiping</strong> |Prevent moisture retention|
| <strong>Check floor material</strong> |Some materials can't be wet-mopped|

<strong>Cleaning by Floor Type</strong>

|Floor Type|Cleaning Method|Precautions|
|---|---|---|
| <strong>Carpet</strong> |Vacuum only|No wet mopping|
| <strong>Wood Flooring</strong> |Well-wrung cloth|No excess water|
| <strong>Cushion Floor</strong> |Well-wrung cloth|Detergent OK|

### Step 4: Wall Wiping (5 Minutes)

<strong>Wall Cleaning Methods</strong>

|Wall Type|Cleaning Method|Precautions|
|---|---|---|
| <strong>Sound-absorbing material (fabric)</strong> |Dry wipe only|No water|
| <strong>Hard panels</strong> |Well-wrung cloth|Wipe gently|
| <strong>Painted surfaces</strong> |Well-wrung cloth|Don't scrub hard|

<strong>Wall Cleaning Tips</strong>

1. <strong>Wipe from top to bottom</strong>
- <strong>Dust falls downward</strong>
- <strong>More efficient</strong>

2. <strong>Focus on frequently touched areas</strong>
- <strong>Around door</strong>
- <strong>Around switches</strong>
- <strong>Lower wall sections</strong>

3. <strong>For heavy soiling</strong>
- <strong>Use diluted neutral detergent</strong>
- <strong>Finish with dry wipe</strong>

### Step 5: Inspection (5 Minutes)

<strong>Visual Inspection Items</strong>

|Inspection Item|Check Method|If Abnormal|
|---|---|---|
| <strong>Ventilation fan filter</strong> |Check dust amount|Clean or replace|
| <strong>Door seal</strong> |Check for cracks/hardening|Record, monitor|
| <strong>Floor condition</strong> |Check for peeling/dents|Record, consult professional|
| <strong>Wall condition</strong> |Check for stains/mold|Apply mold prevention|

<strong>If Abnormality Found</strong>

1. Take photo for record
2. Note date
3. Monitor progress
4. Consult professional if worsens
