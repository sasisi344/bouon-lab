"Can I buy a soundproof room with a ¥500,000 budget?" "What's the best soundproof room for ¥1 million or ¥2 million?"

<strong>Soundproof room selection by budget offers optimal choices for each price range</strong>. For ¥500,000, you can choose basic soundproof booths (like Danbochi). For ¥1 million, used soundproof rooms or small unit types are available. For ¥2 million, you can select professional unit-type soundproof rooms (2-tatami size). However, these are base prices only. Including delivery/installation fees and optional equipment, total additional costs range from ¥200,000 to ¥500,000.

This article provides a comprehensive guide to selecting soundproof rooms by budget in Japan. We cover optimal selection methods, product examples, cost breakdowns, and considerations for ¥500,000, ¥1 million, and ¥2 million budgets.

## How to Choose a Soundproof Room with ¥500,000 Budget

### Types Available with ¥500,000 Budget

With a ¥500,000 budget, you can mainly choose basic soundproof booths.

<strong>Danbochi (Yamaha)</strong>

- <strong>Price:</strong> ¥60,000-150,000
- <strong>Soundproof Performance:</strong> D-30 level (basic)
- <strong>Features:</strong> Cardboard material, one-person assembly, lightweight
- <strong>Suitable Uses:</strong> Streaming, online meetings, light vocal practice, acoustic guitar
- <strong>Total:</strong> Base price only, almost no additional costs

<strong>Imported Basic Booths</strong>

- <strong>Price:</strong> ¥80,000-300,000
- <strong>Soundproof Performance:</strong> D-30-40 level
- <strong>Features:</strong> High design quality, variety of options
- <strong>Suitable Uses:</strong> Streaming, telework, light use

### Selection Points for ¥500,000 Budget

1. <strong>Clarify Intended Use</strong>: Basic soundproof booths are sufficient for streaming/telework
2. <strong>Check Installation Environment</strong>: Confirm if installation is possible in rental properties
3. <strong>Consider Additional Costs</strong>: With ¥500,000 budget, choose products that complete at base price

### Considerations for ¥500,000 Budget

- <strong>Not suitable for serious instrument practice</strong>
- <strong>Ventilation needed for extended use</strong>
- <strong>Limited soundproof performance (D-30-40 level)</strong>

## How to Choose a Soundproof Room with ¥1,000,000 Budget

### Types Available with ¥1,000,000 Budget

With a ¥1,000,000 budget, you can choose used soundproof rooms or small unit types.

<strong>Used Soundproof Room</strong>

- <strong>Price:</strong> 40-70% of new price (¥600,000-1M)
- <strong>Soundproof Performance:</strong> D-40-50 level (equivalent to new)
- <strong>Features:</strong> Good condition units offer same performance as new
- <strong>Considerations:</strong> Dismantling/transport/reassembly costs ¥300,000-800,000
- <strong>Total:</strong> ¥900,000-1.8M (unit + dismantling/transport/reassembly costs)

<strong>Small Unit Type (1.5-tatami)</strong>

- <strong>Price:</strong> ¥980,000-1.3M
- <strong>Manufacturers:</strong> Kawai Nasal (¥980,000+), Yamaha Sefine NS (¥1.1M+)
- <strong>Soundproof Performance:</strong> D-40-50 level
- <strong>Suitable Uses:</strong> Piano (upright), wind instruments, string instruments, vocal
- <strong>Total:</strong> ¥1.2-1.5M (unit + delivery/installation costs)

### Selection Points for ¥1,000,000 Budget

1. <strong>Consider Used Market</strong>: Good condition used units offer significant cost savings
2. <strong>Choose Small Unit Type</strong>: 1.5-tatami size available in ¥1M range
3. <strong>Consider Additional Costs</strong>: Calculate total including delivery/installation fees

### Considerations for ¥1,000,000 Budget

- <strong>Condition check important for used units</strong>
- <strong>Small size not suitable for instruments requiring large space</strong>
- <strong>May exceed budget when including delivery/installation fees</strong>

## Budget-Based Selection: Tips to Reduce Costs

### Used Soundproof Room Utilization

If you don't need new, the used market offers attractive options.

- <strong>Used Market Price:</strong> 40-70% of new price
- <strong>Advantages:</strong> Significant cost savings
- <strong>Considerations:</strong> Dismantling/transport/reassembly costs ¥300,000-800,000

### Loan and Installment Payment Utilization

Many use loans or installment payments for expensive soundproof rooms.

- <strong>Manufacturer Partner Loans:</strong> Interest rate 2-5% annually
- <strong>Payment Example:</strong> For ¥1.5M soundproof room, 36 payments (3 years) at 3% interest = approximately ¥43,700/month

### Subsidy and Grant Utilization

Some municipalities offer subsidies for soundproof construction.

- <strong>Subsidy Amount:</strong> 10-30% of construction cost, cap ¥200,000-500,000
- <strong>Eligible:</strong> Home renovation subsidies, music culture promotion grants
