## The True Nature of Streaming Noise: Why Appliances and Household Sounds Become Noise

### Why Appliances Easily Become Environmental Noise

Appliances (refrigerators, ventilation fans, PC fans) have characteristics that easily make them environmental noise in streaming environments.

<strong>Characteristics of Appliance Noise</strong>

| Appliance Type | Sound Pressure Level | Noise Type | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| <strong>Ventilation Fan</strong> | 40-60dB | Fan rotation sound, wind noise | Constant |
| <strong>Refrigerator</strong> | 35-50dB | Compressor sound, fan sound | Intermittent |
| <strong>PC Fan</strong> | 30-45dB | Fan rotation sound | Constant (under load) |
| <strong>Air Conditioner</strong> | 40-55dB | Fan sound, refrigerant sound | Intermittent |
| <strong>Washing Machine</strong> | 50-70dB | Motor sound, vibration sound | Only when in use |

These appliance noises become distracting noise for viewers in streaming environments, and are particularly fatal for <strong>ASMR streaming</strong> or <strong>streams requiring quiet environments</strong>.

### Impact of Household Sounds on Streaming

Household sounds, family members, and appliance sounds also become distracting noise for viewers.

<strong>Main Types of Household Sounds</strong>

- <strong>Family Conversation:</strong> 30-50dB, mixes with stream audio
- <strong>TV Sound:</strong> 40-60dB, picked up as background noise
- <strong>Door Opening/Closing Sounds:</strong> 50-60dB, noticeable as sudden noise
- <strong>Water Flow Sounds:</strong> 35-45dB, continuous noise
- <strong>Stair Sounds:</strong> 40-50dB, propagates as vibration sound

These household sounds become distracting noise for viewers in streaming environments, degrading stream quality.

### Impact of Noise on Stream Audio Quality

Environmental noise affects stream audio quality in the following ways:

<strong>Impact of Noise on Stream Audio Quality</strong>

- <strong>Reduced Speech Clarity:</strong> Background noise drowns out speech
- <strong>Reduced Viewer Concentration:</strong> Continuous noise disrupts viewer concentration
- <strong>Lower Stream Quality Ratings:</strong> Streams with much noise have lower viewer retention

Especially for <strong>ASMR streaming</strong> or <strong>audio-focused streams</strong>, environmental noise becomes a fatal problem.

<strong>Factors Causing Ventilation Fan Sounds</strong>

During streaming, adjusting how ventilation fans are used can reduce noise.

## Methods to Reduce Noise Through Microphone Settings

### Microphone Directionality Settings

Properly setting microphone directionality can make it less likely to pick up environmental noise.

<strong>How to Choose Microphone Directionality</strong>

- <strong>Cardioid Directionality:</strong> Picks up sounds in front, suppresses sounds from behind/sides (recommended)
- <strong>Super Cardioid Directionality:</strong> Picks up sounds from narrower range (effective when environmental noise is high)
- <strong>Omnidirectional:</strong> Should be avoided (picks up surrounding sounds equally)

Setting to cardioid mode and positioning environmental noise sources behind or to the side of microphone can significantly reduce environmental noise.

### Noise Gate Settings

Utilizing noise gate functionality can automatically cut environmental noise below a certain level.

<strong>Noise Gate Settings in OBS</strong>

1. <strong>Add Filter</strong>: Add "Noise Gate" filter to microphone source
2. <strong>Close Threshold</strong>: Set to -50 to -40dB (below environmental noise level)
3. <strong>Open Threshold</strong>: Set to -35 to -30dB (speech level)
4. <strong>Attack Time</strong>: 5-10ms (reaction speed)
5. <strong>Release Time</strong>: 100-200ms (natural switching)

Noise gates cut environmental noise when not speaking and naturally pass audio when speaking.

### Noise Suppression Filter Settings

Utilizing OBS noise suppression filter can effectively remove environmental noise.

<strong>OBS Noise Suppression Setup</strong>

1. <strong>Add Filter</strong>: Add "Noise Suppression" filter to microphone source
2. <strong>Suppression Level</strong>: Set to moderate (approximately -30dB)
3. <strong>Use RNNoise</strong>: Enables more advanced noise removal (increases CPU usage)

OBS noise suppression effectively removes noise with consistent patterns like environmental noise.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: I'm worried about ventilation if I stop the fan?

<strong>A</strong>: When stopping ventilation fans, the following points require attention:

<strong>Precautions When Stopping Ventilation Fans</strong>

1. <strong>Short Stream Time</strong>: If stream is within 1 hour, ventilating before streaming is fine
2. <strong>Ensure Ventilation</strong>: Ventilate sufficiently before streaming and after streaming
3. <strong>Low Airflow Setting</strong>: Instead of completely stopping, setting to low airflow is also effective
4. <strong>Air Flow</strong>: Open windows to create air flow

For short stream times, ventilating before streaming and stopping the fan during streaming is effective.

### Q2: Can I stop the refrigerator?

<strong>A</strong>: Refrigerators cannot be stopped, but the following countermeasures are effective:

<strong>Refrigerator Noise Countermeasures</strong>

1. <strong>Position Adjustment</strong>: Keep refrigerator away from streaming area
2. <strong>Vibration Isolation Mat</strong>: Install vibration isolation mat under refrigerator
3. <strong>Adjust Stream Time</strong>: Stream when refrigerator operation is minimal
4. <strong>Replace with Quiet Type</strong>: Replace with quiet refrigerator (high cost)

Keeping refrigerator away from streaming area significantly reduces noise.

### Q3: Can household sounds be completely eliminated?

<strong>A</strong>: Completely eliminating household sounds is difficult, but can be significantly reduced with the following countermeasures:

<strong>Household Sound Countermeasures</strong>

1. <strong>Physical Countermeasures</strong>: Isolate streaming area, close doors
2. <strong>Time-Based Countermeasures</strong>: Stream when household sounds are minimal
3. <strong>Microphone Settings</strong>: Noise gate and noise suppression settings
4. <strong>Software Countermeasures</strong>: Utilize RTX Voice and VST plugins

Combining these countermeasures can significantly reduce household sounds.
