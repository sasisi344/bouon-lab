Considering soundproofing renovation but unsure about available subsidies?

Great news! From 2025, official Japanese support programs for soundproofing renovation have expanded significantly. Homeowners of detached houses may qualify for subsidies up to several million yen.

This newsletter details 2025 Japanese subsidy programs, application procedures, and eligibility requirements.

## Japanese Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport & Tourism (MLIT) "Green Renovation Promotion Project"

Most accessible national subsidy program:

### Program Overview

| Item                  | Details                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| <strong>Official Name</strong> | Building Energy-Efficient Renovation Promotion Project |
| <strong>Implementing Body</strong> | Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport & Tourism  |
| <strong>Budget</strong> | ¥60 billion (2025 fiscal year)                         |
| <strong>Eligible Period</strong> | April 2025–March 2026                                  |
| <strong>Maximum Subsidy</strong> | ¥2,000,000                                             |

### Eligible Renovation Types

<strong>When Soundproofing Qualifies</strong>

| Renovation                      | Eligible | Notes                                   |
| ------------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------- |
| <strong>Exterior wall soundproofing</strong> | ◎        | Must simultaneously include insulation  |
| <strong>Window soundproofing</strong> | ◎        | Requires insulation glass               |
| <strong>Roof soundproofing</strong> | △        | Energy efficiency improvement mandatory |
| <strong>Door soundproofing</strong> | ◎        | Sound reduction documented              |

<strong>Critical Conditions</strong>

- <strong>Must simultaneously improve insulation performance</strong>
- <strong>Post-renovation energy efficiency standards must be met</strong>
- <strong>Construction cost minimum ¥500K</strong>

### Subsidy Calculation Method

<strong>Formula</strong>

```
Subsidy = (Construction Cost × Subsidy Rate) ≤ Maximum Subsidy
```

<strong>Subsidy Rates</strong>

| Construction Cost | Subsidy Rate    |
| ----------------- | --------------- |
| ¥500K–¥1M         | 1/3 (max ¥330K) |
| ¥1M–¥2M           | 1/2 (max ¥500K) |
| ¥2M+              | 1/2 (max ¥1M)   |

<strong>Ceiling Amounts</strong>

- <strong>Soundproofing work alone:</strong> Maximum ¥1M
- <strong>Soundproofing + other energy work:</strong> Maximum ¥2M

## Important Considerations for Receiving Subsidies

Key points for successful application:

### Critical Conditions

<strong>① Contractor Selection</strong>

- <strong>Must use "registered contractors"</strong>
- <strong>Non-registered contractors ineligible</strong>
- <strong>Always verify with municipal office</strong>

<strong>② Construction Cost Threshold</strong>

- <strong>Soundproofing work:</strong> Minimum ¥500K
- <strong>Total must meet requirements</strong>
- <strong>Based on post-discount amount</strong>

<strong>③ Timing</strong>

- <strong>Application must be "before construction"</strong>
- <strong>Post-construction application typically impossible</strong>
- <strong>Apply before work begins</strong>

<strong>④ Self-Payment Ratio</strong>

- <strong>Typical subsidy covers ~50% maximum</strong>
- <strong>Minimum 50% self-payment required</strong>
- <strong>Not full coverage</strong>

### Common Pitfalls

<strong>Pitfall ①: Misunderstanding Energy Efficiency Standards</strong>

- <strong>Soundproofing alone ineligible</strong>
- <strong>Must also include energy efficiency improvements</strong>
- <strong>Post-construction inspection required</strong>

<strong>Pitfall ②: Using Non-Registered Contractors</strong>

- <strong>Non-registered contractors create ineligibility</strong>
- <strong>Additional work not recognized</strong>
- <strong>Choose carefully</strong>

<strong>Pitfall ③: Missing Application Deadline</strong>

- <strong>April 2025–March 2026 (planned)</strong>
- <strong>Varies by fiscal year</strong>
- <strong>Confirm with local authority</strong>
