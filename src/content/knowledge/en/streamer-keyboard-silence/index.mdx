Have you ever been told "your keyboard is too loud" during late-night voice chats?
You don't need to buy an expensive silent keyboard; you can drastically reduce noise for a few dollars.

## Why Silencing Keyboard Clicking Matters and How It Works

Keyboard noise consists of two types: "the switch click itself" and "vibration transmitted to the desk."

### Physical Reasons Why Keyboard Noise is Hated in Streams

Microphones easily pick up high-pitched, sharp sounds (clicks), which can be stressful for viewers.
The key is to suppress the "Bottom-out Sound"—the impact sound when you press a key all the way down.

### The Role of Desk Mats as Vibration Dampers

Placing a keyboard directly on a desk turns the entire surface into a speaker for impact sounds.
A thick desk mat acts as a "vibration damper" to absorb this energy.

## Instant Solutions! Silent Rings and Desk Mats

### Under $10 Solution! Hiding Silent Rings Under Keycaps

By removing keys and placing silicone O-rings on the stems, you can eliminate the impact sound of bottoming out.
The feel becomes slightly softer, but it gets dramatically quieter.

### Stopping "Desk Ringing" with Large 4mm+ Desk Mats

Thickness is important for soundproofing, not just mouse stability.
If possible, placing a 100-yen shop joint mat under your desk mat creates the ultimate anti-vibration environment.

## Software Techniques to Remove Keyboard Noise via Mic Settings

In addition to physical measures, try these settings:

- <strong>Noise Suppression (e.g., NVIDIA Broadcast):</strong> AI can clean up clicking sounds perfectly.
- <strong>Adjust Directionality:</strong> Moving the mic away from the keyboard and closer to your mouth (using a mic arm) is the most effective tactic.
