"Mold grew in my soundproof room" "Condensation is severe" "What should I do each season?" If you have these concerns, this guide is for you.

<strong>Soundproof rooms require seasonal priority maintenance. Summer mold prevention and winter condensation control are most critical</strong>. Spring requires pollen/yellow dust measures, and fall requires annual deep cleaning. With appropriate seasonal measures, you can prevent mold and condensation, maintaining an 18-20 year soundproof room.

This article explains seasonal maintenance methods for Japanese soundproof rooms, designed for beginners.

## Why Seasonal Maintenance Matters

Problems soundproof rooms face vary by season.

## Spring Maintenance (March-May): Pollen & Yellow Dust Measures

Spring is when pollen and yellow dust easily enter soundproof rooms.

### Spring Priorities

|Item|Reason|DIY Tasks|Cost|
|---|---|---|---|
| <strong>Filter cleaning</strong> |Pollen/yellow dust measures|Wash ventilation fan filter|¥0|
| <strong>Thorough cleaning</strong> |Remove winter dirt|Wipe entire room|¥0|
| <strong>Dehumidifier preparation</strong> |Prepare for rainy season|Clean/check dehumidifier|¥0|

### Spring Cleaning Steps (3-4 Hours)

<strong>Step 1: Ventilation Fan Filter Cleaning (30 min)</strong>

1. Turn off ventilation fan
2. Remove filter
3. Vacuum dust
4. Wash with neutral detergent
5. Dry completely (24 hours)
6. Reinstall

<strong>Weekly cleaning ideal during high pollen season.</strong>

Step 2: Overall Wiping (2 hours)

1. <strong>Floor wiping</strong>
- <strong>Vacuum</strong>
- <strong>Wipe with well-wrung cloth</strong>
- <strong>Remove winter condensation marks</strong>

2. <strong>Wall wiping</strong>
- <strong>Dry wipe or well-wrung cloth</strong>
- <strong>Wipe from top to bottom</strong>
- <strong>Thoroughly in corners</strong>

3. <strong>Ceiling wiping</strong>
- <strong>Use stepladder</strong>
- <strong>Dry wipe</strong>
- <strong>Remove dust</strong>

<strong>Step 3: Dehumidifier Preparation (30 min)</strong>

1. <strong>Filter cleaning</strong>
- <strong>Vacuum dust</strong>
- <strong>Wash with water if washable</strong>

2. <strong>Water tank cleaning</strong>
- <strong>Wash with neutral detergent</strong>
- <strong>Rinse thoroughly</strong>
- <strong>Dry completely</strong>

3. <strong>Operation check</strong>
- <strong>Power on and check operation</strong>
- <strong>Check for abnormal sounds</strong>

<strong>Step 4: Thorough Ventilation (1 hour)</strong>

- <strong>Ventilate for 1 hour after cleaning</strong>
- <strong>Completely expel moisture</strong>
- <strong>Use air circulator for efficiency</strong>

### Spring Pollen Measures

<strong>Methods to Prevent Pollen Entry</strong>

1. <strong>Ventilation timing</strong>
- <strong>Early morning (6-8 AM) or night (after 8 PM)</strong>
- <strong>Ventilate when pollen is low</strong>

2. <strong>Filter strengthening</strong>
- <strong>Replace with high-performance filter</strong>
- <strong>Pollen-prevention filter</strong>

3. <strong>Shorten ventilation time</strong>
- <strong>Reduce from usual 10 min to 5 min</strong>
- <strong>Increase efficiency with air circulator</strong>

## Summer Maintenance (June-August): Mold Prevention Most Critical

Summer's high temperature and humidity make mold most likely.

### Summer Priorities

|Item|Reason|DIY Tasks|Cost|
|---|---|---|---|
| <strong>Mold prevention treatment</strong> |High temperature/humidity|Apply mold prevention spray|¥1,000-2,000 ($7-14)|
| <strong>Strengthen dehumidification</strong> |Humidity management|Run dehumidifier 24 hours|Increased electricity|
| <strong>Thorough ventilation</strong> |Expel heat/moisture|Always ventilate after each use|¥0|
| <strong>Air conditioner filter cleaning</strong> |Improve efficiency|Wash filter|¥0|

### Summer Mold Prevention Steps (1 Hour)

<strong>Step 1: Overall Dry Wiping (20 min)</strong>

- <strong>Wipe floor, walls, ceiling</strong>
- <strong>Focus on corners where moisture accumulates</strong>
- <strong>Remove dirt</strong>

<strong>Step 2: Mold Prevention Spray Application (20 min)</strong>

1. <strong>Prepare commercial mold prevention spray</strong>
- <strong>Cost:</strong> ¥1,000-2,000 ($7-14)
- <strong>Purchase at home center or drugstore</strong>

2. <strong>Spray on walls, ceiling, floor corners</strong>
- <strong>Especially where moisture accumulates</strong>
- <strong>Use sparingly on sound-absorbing materials</strong>

3. <strong>Ventilate (30 min)</strong>
- <strong>Ventilate thoroughly for 30 minutes</strong>
- <strong>Dry mold prevention agent</strong>
- <strong>Use air circulator</strong>

4. <strong>Run dehumidifier (24 hours)</strong>
- <strong>Run dehumidifier 24 hours</strong>
- <strong>Maintain humidity below 50%</strong>
- <strong>Empty water tank daily</strong>

### Mold Prevention Spray Selection

|Item|Recommendation|
|---|---|
| <strong>Type</strong> |Alcohol-based dries quickly|
| <strong>Cost</strong> |¥1,000-2,000 ($7-14)|
| <strong>Where to buy</strong> |Home center, drugstore|
| <strong>Specialized</strong> |"Soundproof room specific" unnecessary (general mold prevention spray sufficient)|

### Effective Dehumidifier Use

<strong>Dehumidifier Settings</strong>

|Item|Recommended Setting|
|---|---|
| <strong>Operating time</strong> |24 hours|
| <strong>Target humidity</strong> |Below 50%|
| <strong>Water tank</strong> |Empty daily|
| <strong>Electricity cost</strong> |About ¥3,000-5,000 ($20-35) monthly|

<strong>Dehumidifier Selection</strong>

- <strong>Match soundproof room size</strong>

### Summer Mold Prevention Checklist

```
□ 10-minute ventilation after each use
□ Weekly mold prevention spray

□ Maintain humidity below 50%
```

Fall is the ideal season for annual deep cleaning.

### Fall Thorough Cleaning Steps (3-4 Hours)

<strong>Step 1: Mold Check (30 min)</strong>

1. <strong>Check ceiling, walls, floor corners</strong>
- <strong>Illuminate with flashlight</strong>
- <strong>Check for black spots</strong>

2. <strong>Assess mold severity</strong>
- <strong>Mild:</strong> Surface only
- <strong>Moderate:</strong> Wide area
- <strong>Severe:</strong> Penetrated inside

<strong>Step 2: Mold Removal Work (1 hour)</strong>

Mild Mold Removal Steps

1. Prepare rubbing alcohol (¥500-1,000 / $3-7)
2. Soak cloth
3. Wipe moldy areas
4. Ventilate 30 minutes to dry
5. Apply mold prevention spray

<strong>Moderate Mold Removal Steps</strong>

1. Prepare mold remover (chlorine-based, ¥1,000-2,000 / $7-14)
2. Spray mold remover
3. Leave 5-10 minutes
4. Wet wipe → dry wipe
5. Dry completely for 24+ hours
6. Apply mold prevention spray

<strong>Severe Mold</strong>

- <strong>Difficult to handle yourself</strong>
- <strong>Consult professional (component replacement needed)</strong>
- <strong>Cost:</strong> ¥50,000+ ($340+)

2. <strong>Wall wiping</strong>
- <strong>Dry wipe or well-wrung cloth</strong>
- <strong>Wipe from top to bottom</strong>
- <strong>Thoroughly in corners</strong>

3. <strong>Ceiling wiping</strong>
- <strong>Use stepladder</strong>
- <strong>Dry wipe</strong>
- <strong>Remove mold marks</strong>

<strong>Step 4: Humidifier Preparation (30 min)</strong>

1. <strong>Filter cleaning</strong>
- <strong>Vacuum dust</strong>
- <strong>Wash with water</strong>

2. <strong>Water tank cleaning</strong>
- <strong>Wash with neutral detergent</strong>
- <strong>Rinse thoroughly</strong>
- <strong>Dry completely</strong>

3. <strong>Operation check</strong>
- <strong>Power on and check operation</strong>

## Winter Maintenance (December-February): Condensation Control Most Critical

Winter brings condensation and dryness issues.

### Winter Priorities

|Item|Reason|DIY Tasks|Cost|
|---|---|---|---|
| <strong>Humidification management</strong> |Dryness measures|Run humidifier|Increased electricity|
| <strong>Condensation wiping</strong> |Mold prevention|Wipe condensation every morning|¥0|
| <strong>Static electricity measures</strong> |Comfort|Address with humidification|¥0|

### Winter Humidification Management (Daily)

<strong>Step 1: Maintain 40-50% Humidity</strong>

1. <strong>Run humidifier</strong>
- <strong>24-hour operation ideal</strong>
- <strong>Check with hygrometer</strong>

2. <strong>Check with hygrometer</strong>
- <strong>Target humidity:</strong> 40-50%
- <strong>Watch for over-drying</strong>

3. <strong>Beware of over-humidification</strong>
- <strong>Over 60% causes condensation</strong>
- <strong>Maintain appropriate humidity</strong>

<strong>Step 2: Condensation Wiping (Every Morning 10 min)</strong>

1. <strong>Check wall/ceiling condensation</strong>
- <strong>Focus especially on corners</strong>
- <strong>Touch to check for moisture</strong>

2. <strong>Wipe with dry cloth</strong>
- <strong>Wipe immediately</strong>
- <strong>Leaving causes mold</strong>

3. <strong>Ventilation timing</strong>
- <strong>Ventilate on sunny days during midday</strong>
- <strong>Avoid morning/evening (condensation-prone)</strong>
- <strong>10 minutes sufficient</strong>

<strong>Step 3: Ventilation Timing</strong>

|Time|Ventilate|Reason|
|---|---|---|
| <strong>Morning (6-8 AM)</strong> |×|Outside air cold, condensation-prone|
| <strong>Midday (12-2 PM)</strong> |○|Outside air warm, condensation-resistant|
| <strong>Evening (after 6 PM)</strong> |×|Outside air cold, condensation-prone|

### Humidifier Selection

|Item|Recommendation|
|---|---|
| <strong>Type</strong> |Ultrasonic or steam type|
| <strong>Cost</strong> |¥10,000-30,000 ($70-200)|
| <strong>Tank capacity</strong> |Large is convenient (4L+)|
| <strong>Function</strong> |Humidity setting function convenient|

### Condensation Control Points

<strong>Condensation-Prone Locations</strong>

- <strong>Ceiling corners</strong>
- <strong>Wall corners</strong>
- <strong>Windows (if present)</strong>
- <strong>Around ventilation fan</strong>

<strong>If Condensation Left Untreated</strong>

- <strong>Mold grows</strong>
- <strong>Wall materials deteriorate</strong>
- <strong>Sound insulation performance declines</strong>
- <strong>Odors develop</strong>

<strong>Morning condensation wiping is winter's most important task.</strong>
