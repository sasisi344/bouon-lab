\"I want to use the soundproof room I bought even at my new home!\"

However, when you ask a moving company for a quote, it's not rare to be refused, with them saying <strong>"We cannot handle soundproof rooms."</strong>
How much does it cost to relocate in the first place? Will the performance drop? It's full of anxiety, isn't it?

As a Kind Life Advisor, I'll tell you the costs for relocating a soundproof room and tips for a smooth move in this article.

## Total Relocation Cost: 150,000 to 400,000 JPY is the Market Rate

![Breakdown of relocation costs](relocation-cost-breakdown.jpg)

Relocating a soundproof room incurs the following three costs:

| Item | Market Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| <strong>Disassembly Fee</strong> | 30,000 - 80,000 JPY |
| <strong>Transportation Fee</strong> | 50,000 - 200,000 JPY (Varies by distance) |
| <strong>Reassembly Fee</strong> | 50,000 - 120,000 JPY |
| <strong>Total</strong> | <strong>150,000 - 400,000 JPY</strong> |

*For a 1.5-mat unit soundproof room. Varies by size and distance.

## Reasons Why General Moving Companies Cannot Handle It

You might think "Won't it be cheaper if I ask a regular moving company?", but most companies refuse.

### Reason 1: Specialized Knowledge Is Required

A soundproof room is not just a combination of panels.
If the <strong>direction of the sound-insulating gaskets (rubber parts)</strong> or the <strong>tightening torque</strong> is wrong, the performance will drop significantly. General moving staff cannot handle it.

### Reason 2: Heavy Objects & Damage Risk

A single panel of a soundproof room weighs 20kg to 30kg.
Without specialized tools and experience, there is a risk of scratching walls and floors or damaging the panel itself.

## Solution: Ask a Soundproof Room Specialist

For Yamaha or Kawai soundproof rooms, there are official manufacturer relocation services (or affiliated companies).

### For Yamaha Avitecs:

- <strong>Contact</strong>: Request via the instrument store where you purchased it.
- <strong>Estimated Cost</strong>: From 150,000 JPY for short distances (within the same prefecture).
- <strong>Benefit</strong>: Performance guarantee exists, including gasket replacement.

### Unofficial Specialists:

- <strong>Cost</strong>: Sometimes 20-30% cheaper than official ones.
- <strong>Note</strong>: There may be no performance guarantee after relocation.

## Risks and Countermeasures for Performance Degradation During Relocation

### Risk 1: Gasket Deterioration

Once disassembled, the sound-insulation gaskets (rubber parts) can deteriorate, causing gaps.

<strong>Countermeasure</strong>: Ask the relocation specialist for <strong>"gasket replacement"</strong> (additional cost of 10,000 to 30,000 JPY).

### Risk 2: Gaps During Reassembly

If the assembly is poor, sound leaks from the gaps between panels.

<strong>Countermeasure</strong>: <strong>Always have a performance check</strong> done after relocation. Or do a sound leakage test yourself (have someone listen outside).

## Air Conditioner Removal and Installation Problem

If you have an air conditioner dedicated to the soundproof room, you need to arrange an <strong>air conditioner contractor</strong> separately.

- <strong>Removal/Installation Fee</strong>: 15,000 to 30,000 JPY (higher if piping extension is needed).
- <strong>Timing</strong>: It is necessary to remove the air conditioner <strong>before</strong> the disassembly of the soundproof room.

<strong>Important</strong>: Schedule adjustment between the soundproof room company and the air conditioner company is mandatory.

## What to Communicate when Getting a Quote

When requesting a quote from a moving company (or a soundproof room specialist), be sure to communicate the following:

- <strong>Manufacturer & Model Number</strong> (e.g., Yamaha Avitecs Cefine NS 1.5 Mats)
- <strong>Installation Floor</strong> (If it's on the 2nd floor or above, costs increase for crane use)
- <strong>Presence of an Air Conditioner</strong>
- <strong>Installation Location at the New Home</strong> (width of delivery route, presence of an elevator)

## Summary: Relocation vs. Sale & New Purchase, Which Is a Better Deal?

| Relocation Cost | New Purchase Price |
| :--- | :--- |
| 150,000 - 400,000 JPY | 500,000 - 1,000,000 JPY |

If the relocation cost exceeds 300,000 yen, it's also worth considering the option of <strong>"selling the current soundproof room and buying a used one at the new home."</strong>
