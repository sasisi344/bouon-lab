## How to Choose Soundproof Materials: Absorption vs. Soundproofing

### Understanding Absorption and Soundproofing Materials

Soundproof materials are broadly divided into two categories: "absorption materials" and "soundproofing materials". Let's understand the role of each.

<strong>Absorption Materials: Materials that weaken sound</strong>

- <strong>Role</strong>: Reduce indoor reverberation and eliminate sound muffling
- <strong>Mechanism</strong>: Sound waves enter fine pores in the material and are converted to heat through friction with air
- <strong>Effect</strong>: Improves indoor sound quality, prevents echo
- <strong>Examples</strong>: Glass wool, urethane sponge, acoustic panels

<strong>Soundproofing Materials: Materials that block sound</strong>

- <strong>Role</strong>: Prevent external sound from entering, prevent internal sound from leaking out
- <strong>Mechanism</strong>: Material mass blocks sound waves and prevents transmission
- <strong>Effect</strong>: Prevents sound leakage, blocks external noise
- <strong>Examples</strong>: Soundproofing sheets, gypsum board, concrete

<strong>Basic Usage</strong>

In most cases, you'll combine them in the order "soundproofing → absorption". First, use soundproofing materials to prevent sound from entering/exiting, then use absorption materials to improve indoor sound quality.

### Material Selection by Purpose

<strong>For Streaming/Telework</strong>

- <strong>Priority</strong>: Absorption materials > Soundproofing materials
- <strong>Reason</strong>: Preventing indoor reverberation and echo is top priority
- <strong>Recommended Materials</strong>: Urethane sponge, acoustic panels, soundproof curtains
- <strong>Budget Guide</strong>: ¥30,000-¥100,000 ($200-700 USD)

<strong>For Musical Instruments</strong>

- <strong>Priority</strong>: Soundproofing materials > Absorption materials
- <strong>Reason</strong>: Preventing sound leakage is top priority, then sound quality adjustment
- <strong>Recommended Materials</strong>: Soundproofing sheets, gypsum board, glass wool
- <strong>Budget Guide</strong>: ¥100,000-¥500,000 ($700-3,500 USD)

<strong>For Rental Properties (Restorable)</strong>

- <strong>Priority</strong>: Simple absorption materials > Soundproof curtains
- <strong>Reason</strong>: Choose materials that don't require drilling holes and can be removed
- <strong>Recommended Materials</strong>: Acoustic panels (no adhesive needed), soundproof curtains, simple soundproof mats
- <strong>Budget Guide</strong>: ¥30,000-¥150,000 ($200-1,000 USD)

## Important Notes When Purchasing Soundproof Materials

### Size/Quantity Calculation Errors

<strong>Common Mistakes</strong>

- Not accurately calculating required area, resulting in insufficient materials
- Purchasing materials wastefully without considering doors and windows

<strong>Solutions</strong>

- Create a design drawing and calculate accurate area
- Purchase with 10-20% margin
- Compare multiple sizes and choose the optimal size

### Quality Variations

<strong>Common Mistakes</strong>

- Choosing low-priced materials but quality is low and effectiveness is limited
- Purchasing online but actual product differs from image

<strong>Solutions</strong>

- Check reviews to understand actual user experiences
- If possible, obtain samples to verify
- Consult specialty stores to select materials appropriate for your purpose

### Installation Difficulty

<strong>Common Mistakes</strong>

- Choosing materials that are difficult to install, making DIY difficult
- Lacking tools, preventing progress

<strong>Solutions</strong>

- Check installation methods in advance and judge if you can handle with your skills
- List required tools and prepare in advance
- Consider consulting professionals for difficult parts
