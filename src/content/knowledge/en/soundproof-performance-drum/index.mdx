"If I buy a unit soundproof room, I can play the drums at home!"
If you think so, please stop and think once.

I will tell you the cruel reality.
<strong>If you play real drums in a commercially available assembly-type soundproof room (Dr-35 to 40), the police will be called by neighbors immediately.</strong>

Drum soundproofing is in a different dimension from other instruments. I will explain why it is so difficult and what you can do to be able to play.

## Two Giant Enemies of Drum Soundproofing

### 1. Explosive Sound Pressure (110dB to 120dB)

The volume of a drum set is the same level as near the engine of a jet plane.
Even if you use a Dr-40 soundproof room, the sound "70dB to 80dB" leaks outside. This is the same level as "<strong>noisy streets</strong>" or "<strong>close range of cicadas chirping</strong>".
If this is heard in a quiet residential area, it will undoubtedly become a noise problem.

### 2. Low-frequency Vibration that Shakes the Ground (Solid-borne Sound)

This is the true boss.
When you step on the bass drum (kick), an impact like a person jumping with a thud is transmitted to the floor.
This "thud!" vibration travels through the concrete of the building and echoes not only in the <strong>neighboring house but also in all rooms upstairs, downstairs, and diagonally in the apartment</strong>.

Even if you block the air with a wall, the vibration will not stop.

## Is it Impossible to play Drums in a Unit Soundproof Room?

Basically, "<strong>impossible</strong>".
However, there is a possibility with "electronic drums" or "custom-made models" under the following conditions.

- <strong>Ordinary Units (Dr-35/40):</strong>
- <strong>Real Drums:</strong> Not possible.
- <strong>Electronic Drums:</strong> Since the sound of hitting (pad sound) and the vibration of the pedal echo downstairs, a powerful anti-vibration stage such as the "Disk Funya Funya System" is required.
- <strong>High Insulation Units (Custom-made products such as Dr-50):</strong>
- <strong>Yamaha and Kawai have custom-made soundproof rooms dedicated to drums. Real drums are possible with this, but the price jumps to $30,000 or more.</strong>

## The Only Solution to Play Real Drums: Studio Construction (Dr-65)

If you play real drums at home, "<strong>soundproof construction (renovation)</strong>" is required instead of a unit.
And it's not just any construction.

### Create a "Concrete Room Floating Inside a Room"

1.  <strong>Floating Floor (Floating Sound Insulation Layer)</strong>:
- <strong>Float the floor together with the concrete using rubber or springs.</strong>
2.  <strong>Complete Insulation of Walls and Ceilings</strong>:
- <strong>Create a state where the inside room does not touch anywhere in the building.</strong>
3.  <strong>Stop by Mass</strong>:
- <strong>Pile up heavy concrete blocks etc., and stop sound by physical weight.</strong>

Only by doing this can performance of "<strong>Dr-65 to Dr-70</strong>" be produced.
The cost is $40,000 or more for 6 tatami, and it is common for it to cost $50,000 or more for an apartment.

## Summary: "Soundproofing + Anti-vibration" is Essential to Play Drums in Collective Housing

Even unit-type soundproof rooms considerably reduce the "sound (ringing)" itself transmitted through the air.
However, the true enemy in drums is the "<strong>vibration (solid-borne sound)</strong>" where the impact of stepping on the pedal travels through the floor.

- <strong>In the case of a Detached House (1st floor):</strong>
- <strong>There is a high possibility that neighboring trouble can be prevented by just introducing a unit soundproof room.</strong>
- <strong>In the case of an Apartment (2nd floor or higher):</strong>
- <strong>This is the biggest hurdle. In addition to soundproofing the wall, "<strong>powerful anti-vibration measures (floating floors, etc.)" that physically block the impact transmitted to the floor are indispensable.</strong></strong>

If you play drums in collective housing, please consider "stopping the vibration to the floor" as a set, not just "stopping the sound".
Easy countermeasures will lead to trouble, but as long as a correct anti-vibration structure can be made, it is not impossible to enjoy drums at home.
