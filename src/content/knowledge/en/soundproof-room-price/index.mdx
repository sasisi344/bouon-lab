## Price Differences by Performance (Dr-value)

![Price image by performance](price-performance.jpg)

The performance of a soundproof room is expressed by the <strong>"Dr-value (decibels)"</strong>.
The higher the value, the higher the soundproofing performance (= the less sound leaks).

| Dr-value | Performance Image | Estimated Price (1.5 Mats) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| <strong>Dr-30</strong> | Ordinary conversation can be heard. Piano sound is halved | 600,000 - 800,000 JPY |
| <strong>Dr-35</strong> | Piano sound is "faintly" heard. For general streaming | 800,000 - 1,000,000 JPY |
| <strong>Dr-40</strong> | Piano sound is almost unheard. For full-scale instrument practice | 1,000,000 - 1,500,000 JPY |

<strong>Conclusion</strong>: For every increase of 5 in the Dr-value, the price goes up by approximately 200,000 yen.

## Price Range by Manufacturer

### Yamaha "Avitecs" (No. 1 Market Share)

- <strong>Features</strong>: High quality, extensive maintenance system
- <strong>Price</strong>: Slightly higher (900,000 - 1,200,000 JPY for 1.5 mats)
- <strong>Recommendation</strong>: Long-term use, professionals

### Kawai "Nasal"

- <strong>Features</strong>: 20-30% cheaper than Yamaha
- <strong>Price</strong>: Focus on cost-performance (700,000 - 1,000,000 JPY for 1.5 mats)
- <strong>Recommendation</strong>: For those who want to keep the budget down

### VERYQ / "Danbotchi"

- <strong>Features</strong>: Super compact, DIY assembly
- <strong>Price</strong>: Extremely cheap (100,000 - 150,000 JPY for less than 1 mat)
- <strong>Caution</strong>: Soundproofing performance is low (around Dr-25)

## Hidden Costs: Installation and Delivery Fees

It's not just the price of the soundproof room unit itself. The following <strong>additional costs</strong> will definitely occur.

| Item | Cost Market |
| :--- | :--- |
| <strong>Delivery/Assembly Fee</strong> | 50,000 - 100,000 JPY |
| <strong>Air Conditioner Installation</strong> | 100,000 - 200,000 JPY (Dedicated model) |
| <strong>Vibration Mat</strong> | 10,000 - 30,000 JPY |
| <strong>Additional Lighting</strong> | 10,000 - 50,000 JPY |

<strong>Important</strong>: Make sure to check the <strong>"Total Cost (All-in-one)"</strong> when getting an estimate.

## Choosing the Right Soundproof Room for Your Budget

### Budget: Under 300,000 JPY

- <strong>Options</strong>: Danbotchi, used 0.5 mat units
- <strong>Usage</strong>: Streaming, narration recording

### Budget: 500,000 - 800,000 JPY

- <strong>Options</strong>: Yamaha 0.8 mat (Dr-30), Kawai 1.2 mat
- <strong>Usage</strong>: Electronic drums, guitar, piano (upright)

### Budget: 1,000,000 JPY or more

- <strong>Options</strong>: Yamaha 1.5 mats or larger (Dr-35 to Dr-40)
- <strong>Usage</strong>: Full-scale instrument practice, recording

## Summary: Experience It at a Showroom First

A soundproof room cannot be judged by <strong>"numbers (Dr-value)"</strong> alone.
It is most important to actually go inside and check the resonance, the sense of enclosure, and usability.

Yamaha and Kawai both have showrooms nationwide, so first make a reservation and visit them.
