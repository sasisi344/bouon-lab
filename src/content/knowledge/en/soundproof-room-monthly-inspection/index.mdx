## Category 1: Ventilation/Air Conditioning Inspection (15 Minutes)

Ventilation equipment is crucial for soundproof room lifespan.

### Inspection Items and Methods

|Item|Check Method|Normal State|Abnormal Response|
|---|---|---|---|
|<strong>Ventilation fan sound</strong>|Turn on and listen|Quiet rotation|Clean filter|
|<strong>Ventilation fan airflow</strong>|Feel with hand|Strong airflow|Clean filter|
|<strong>Filter dirt</strong>|Remove and inspect|Little dust|Wash with water|
|<strong>Air conditioner effectiveness</strong>|Test heating/cooling|Works immediately|Clean filter|
|<strong>Condensation presence</strong>|Touch walls/ceiling|Not wet|Strengthen dehumidification|

### Ventilation Fan Filter Cleaning Method

<strong>Cleaning Steps (30 minutes)</strong>

1. <strong>Turn off ventilation fan</strong>
   - Always cut power for safety

2. <strong>Remove filter</strong>
   - Screw or clip type
   - Check instruction manual

3. <strong>Vacuum dust</strong>
   - Remove surface dust
   - Vacuum both sides

4. <strong>Wash with neutral detergent</strong>
   - Bucket with water and detergent
   - Wash gently
   - Rinse thoroughly

5. <strong>Dry completely (24 hours)</strong>
   - Dry in shade
   - Wait until completely dry

6. <strong>Reinstall</strong>
   - Secure firmly
   - Check operation

<strong>Cleaning frequency: Monthly</strong>

### Air Conditioner Filter Cleaning Method

<strong>Cleaning Steps (15 minutes)</strong>

1. Open air conditioner cover
2. Remove filter
3. Vacuum dust
4. Wash with water if heavily soiled
5. Dry completely
6. Reinstall

<strong>Cleaning frequency: Monthly</strong>

### Condensation Check Method

<strong>Check Locations</strong>

- Ceiling corners
- Wall corners
- Windows (if present)
- Around ventilation fan

<strong>If Condensation Found</strong>

1. Wipe with dry cloth
2. Run dehumidifier 24 hours
3. Strengthen ventilation
4. Reduce temperature difference

## Category 2: Door/Seal Inspection (15 Minutes)

Doors and seals are essential for sound insulation performance.

### Inspection Items and Methods

|Item|Check Method|Normal State|Abnormal Response|
|---|---|---|---|
|<strong>Door opening/closing</strong>|Actually open/close|Smooth|Oil hinges|
|<strong>Door seal</strong>|Paper insertion test|Paper won't pull out|Record, monitor|
|<strong>Seal condition</strong>|Visual, touch|Soft, no cracks|Record, consider replacement|
|<strong>Doorknob looseness</strong>|Check wobble|No wobble|Tighten screws|

### Paper Insertion Test Method

<strong>Test Steps</strong>

1. <strong>Prepare A4 paper</strong>
   - Copy paper is fine

2. <strong>Insert paper in door gap</strong>
   - Test at 4 locations: top, bottom, left, right

3. <strong>Close door</strong>
   - Close normally

4. <strong>Pull paper</strong>
   - Pull with force

<strong>Evaluation</strong>

|Result|Assessment|Response|
|---|---|---|
|<strong>Won't pull out</strong>|Normal (good seal)|No problem|
|<strong>Some resistance</strong>|Monitor|Recheck in 3 months|
|<strong>Pulls out easily</strong>|Seal deterioration|Consider professional replacement|

<strong>Seal replacement cost: ¥30,000-80,000 ($200-540)</strong>

### Seal Condition Check

<strong>Visual Check</strong>

- Any cracks
- Any discoloration
- Any peeling

<strong>Touch Check</strong>

- Press with finger to check elasticity
- Not hardened
- No stickiness

<strong>Deterioration Signs</strong>

|Sign|Condition|Response|
|---|---|---|
|<strong>Hardening</strong>|Lost elasticity|Consider replacement within 1 year|
|<strong>Cracks</strong>|Surface cracks|Consider replacement within 6 months|
|<strong>Deformation</strong>|Shape collapsed|Consider immediate replacement|

### Door Hinge Oiling Method

<strong>Oiling Steps (10 minutes)</strong>

1. Prepare commercial lubricant spray (¥500-1,000 / $3-7)
2. Spray into hinge gaps
3. Open/close door about 10 times
4. Wipe excess oil

<strong>Lubricant to Use</strong>

- Silicone spray (odorless, recommended)
- General lubricant spray
- Cost: ¥500-1,000 ($3-7)

<strong>Oiling frequency: Annually</strong>

## Category 3: Lighting/Electrical Inspection (15 Minutes)

Safety check for lighting and electrical equipment.

### Inspection Items and Methods

|Item|Check Method|Normal State|Abnormal Response|
|---|---|---|---|
|<strong>Lighting brightness</strong>|Turn on and check|Sufficiently bright|Replace LED|
|<strong>Lighting flicker</strong>|Behavior when on|No flicker|Replace LED|
|<strong>Outlet condition</strong>|Plug in/out|Firmly fixed|Consult professional|
|<strong>Wiring condition</strong>|Visual check|No damage|Consult professional|

### LED Lighting Replacement Method (DIY Possible)

<strong>Replacement Steps (15-30 minutes)</strong>

1. <strong>Safety check</strong>
   - Turn off breaker
   - Stabilize stepladder

2. <strong>Remove old lighting</strong>
   - Remove lighting cover
   - Remove lighting unit
   - Check wiring (take photo)

3. <strong>Install new lighting</strong>
   - Connect wiring
   - Secure lighting unit
   - Replace cover

4. <strong>Operation check</strong>
   - Turn on breaker
   - Check lighting

<strong>Required Tools and Costs</strong>

|Item|Cost|
|---|---|
|<strong>LED lighting</strong>|¥5,000-15,000 ($35-100)|
|<strong>Screwdriver</strong>|Use existing|
|<strong>Stepladder</strong>|¥3,000-8,000 ($20-55) if needed|

<strong>DIY Benefits</strong>

- Professional service: ¥15,000-30,000 ($100-200)
- DIY replacement: ¥5,000-15,000 ($35-100)
- <strong>Savings: ¥10,000-15,000 ($70-100)</strong>

### Outlet/Wiring Check

<strong>Outlet Check</strong>

1. Plug in and out
2. Firmly secured
3. No looseness
4. No burning smell

<strong>Wiring Check</strong>

1. Visual inspection for damage
2. No exposed insulation
3. No discoloration

<strong>If Abnormality Found</strong>

- Don't repair yourself
- Electrical work requires qualifications
- Consult professional immediately

## Category 4: Panel/Structure Inspection (15 Minutes)

Check soundproof room structural components.

### Inspection Items and Methods

|Item|Check Method|Normal State|Abnormal Response|
|---|---|---|---|
|<strong>Panel gaps</strong>|Visual, light leak check|No gaps|Record, consult professional|
|<strong>Joint looseness</strong>|Push with hand|No wobble|Tighten screws|
|<strong>Floor sinking</strong>|Walk around|No sinking|Record, consult professional|
|<strong>Wall material peeling</strong>|Visual, touch|No peeling|Record, consult professional|

### Panel Gap Check

<strong>Check Method</strong>

1. Check inside soundproof room during daytime
2. When outside is bright
3. Darken inside of room
4. Check for light leaks

<strong>If Gaps Found</strong>

- Take photo for record
- Consult professional
- May need additional sealant

### Joint Screw Tightening Method

<strong>Tightening Steps (30 minutes)</strong>

1. <strong>Prepare tool set</strong>
   - Screwdriver set (¥1,000-3,000 / $7-20)

2. <strong>Check loose screws</strong>
   - Push with hand to check wobble
   - Focus on joint sections

3. <strong>Tighten with appropriate screwdriver</strong>
   - Use correct size screwdriver
   - Don't overtighten (causes damage)

4. <strong>Check overall wobble</strong>
   - Push with hand
   - Make sounds to check

<strong>Tightening frequency: Every 6 months</strong>

### Floor Sinking/Tilting Check

<strong>Check Method</strong>

1. Walk around inside soundproof room
2. Check for sinking areas
3. Check for tilting sensation

<strong>If Abnormality Found</strong>

- Take photo for record
- Note date
- Consult professional
- May need floor reinforcement

## Environmental Measurement (10 Minutes)

Measure and record temperature and humidity.

### Measurement Items and Target Values

|Item|Target Value|Tool|Abnormal Response|
|---|---|---|---|
|<strong>Temperature</strong>|20-26°C (68-79°F)|Hygrometer|Adjust air conditioning|
|<strong>Humidity</strong>|40-60%|Hygrometer|Dehumidify/humidify|

### Hygrometer Selection

|Item|Recommendation|
|---|---|
|<strong>Type</strong>|Digital is easy to read|
|<strong>Cost</strong>|¥2,000-5,000 ($15-35)|
|<strong>Function</strong>|Wall-mount type is convenient|
|<strong>Recording</strong>|Recording function shows trends|

### Temperature/Humidity Recording Method

<strong>How to Record</strong>

1. <strong>Photo with smartphone</strong>
   - Photograph hygrometer daily at same time
   - Auto-backup with Google Photos etc.

2. <strong>Notebook record</strong>
   - Write date and temperature/humidity
   - Note abnormalities in remarks

3. <strong>Spreadsheet management</strong>
   - Enter in Excel or spreadsheet
   - Visualize trends with graphs

<strong>Recording Benefits</strong>

- Notice abnormal changes immediately
- Understand seasonal trends
- Confirm effectiveness of measures
