## 🚀 The Short Answer
"Acoustic Treatment" (fixing your audio quality) and "Soundproofing" (fixing your neighbor relationship) are completely different solutions.
- <strong>Acoustic Treatment</strong>: Soft panels to kill the "echo" (reverb) in your mic. Even cheap foam can help audio quality, but it will zero-effect on noise leaking out.
- <strong>Soundproofing</strong>: Blocking screaming or game audio completely. Requires Mass and Air Gaps.
- <strong>VTuber Standard</strong>: Acoustic panels + Microphone Shield + Vibration floor mats.

## 1. Acoustic Panels vs. Soundproofing (Clearing the Mystery)
<strong>Context:</strong> The common mistake made by streamers worldwide.
Amazon foam panels won't stop your neighbor's wall-pounding because they lack mass. 
- <strong>Acoustic Treatment</strong>: Absorbing energy to shorten decay time (for that "crispy" close-mic sound).
- <strong>Sound Isolation</strong>: Compressing high-density layers to prevent sound energy from escaping.

## 2. Low-Budget Strategies for Streaming (Survival DIY)
<strong>Context:</strong> Budget hacks for new streamers.
- Sealing the door gaps (Door Sweeps, Weatherstripping) is the #1 cheapest fix.
- Using a "Vocal Shield" behind the mic combined with heavy curtains behind the chair.
- High-pile carpets + Rubber gym mats to kill footfalls and desk thumps.
