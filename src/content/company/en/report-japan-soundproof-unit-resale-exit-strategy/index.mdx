# （EN Persona: Investor/Importer）Soundproof Units as Moveable Assets: A 5-Year Exit Strategy Simulation

<strong>Executive Summary:</strong>
In the Japanese secondary market, <strong>Soundproof Unit Rooms</strong> (Yamaha Cefine, Kawai Nasal) are treated similarly to high-end musical instruments or luxury watches—possessing high liquidity and predictable depreciation curves. For global investors or importers, understanding this "Secondary Market Price Floor" is essential for calculating the true <strong>Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)</strong>.

---

## 1. The Asset Class: Why "Units" Outperform "Construction"
Unlike built-in soundproofing (which is often valued at zero lors of demolition), unit rooms are <strong>independent assets</strong> that can be disassembled, transported, and resold.

### 🔍 Key Market Driver: The "Used Price Floor"
A used 1.5-tatami Yamaha unit rarely drops below ¥300,000, regardless of age, provided the panels are intact. This creates a safety net for the initial investment.

## 3. Exit Strategy: Maximizing Residual Value
To ensure the 50% resale target, the following <strong>Maintenance Protocols</strong> are standard in the Japanese market:

1.  <strong>Non-Smoking Environment:</strong> Nicotine staining on acoustic cloth can reduce resale value by 15-20%.
2.  <strong>Original Documentation:</strong> Keeping the "Assembly Certificate" from authorized technicians is mandatory for high-tier buybacks.
3.  <strong>Climate Control:</strong> Maintaining 40-60% humidity prevents wood-panel warping, crucial for tight seals in the Dr-40/45 models.

## 4. Financial Simulation: Rent vs. Invest (TCO)
For a 1.5-tatami Yamaha Cefine NS over 60 months:

- <strong>Initial Capital:</strong> ¥1,100,000 (including installation)
- <strong>5-Year Resale (Exit):</strong> -¥500,000
- <strong>Net Cost:</strong> ¥600,000
- <strong>Effective Monthly Cost:</strong> <strong>¥10,000 / month</strong>

*Comparison:* Renting a professional studio for 10 hours a month in Tokyo costs approx. <strong>¥30,000 / month</strong>.

## 5. Global Importer Opportunity
The high <strong>Resale-to-MSRP ratio</strong> suggests that the Japanese market can absorb a higher volume of "Certified Pre-owned" units. There is a significant gap for "Subscription-to-Own" models where the unit's residual value acts as the collateral.
