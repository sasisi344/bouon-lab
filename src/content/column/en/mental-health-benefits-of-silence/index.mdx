"I just want a quiet place."

The noise of a café, sounds from neighbors, cars passing by—in today's society, obtaining true "silence" has become more of a luxury than ever. However, what people who install soundproof rooms often say is that the value of <strong>"completely shutting out the outside world"</strong> is just as important as the fact that the sound of their instruments doesn't leak out.

In this column, we reflect on the staggering impact that a soundproof room has on mental health.

---

## 1. Shutting Down "Brain Background Processing"

Even when we aren't aware of it, our brains are constantly monitoring surrounding sounds. This was a survival instinct from the hunter-gatherer era to detect predators. However, in today's noisy environment, this instinct backfires, causing the brain to repeat <strong>"micro-stress responses."</strong>

Entering a soundproof room—a <strong>"sanctuary where you can be physically alone"</strong>—is nothing less than a forced shutdown of these background processes. The sensation of "the brain getting lighter" the moment the sound disappears brings a deep relaxation effect similar to meditation.

---

## 2. "Psychological Safety": The Freedom to Not Be Heard

The value of a soundproof room isn't just "sound insulation." The <strong>security of knowing your voice or sounds aren't leaking out</strong> provides powerful psychological safety.

- Singing your heart out.
- Venting your emotions.
- Developing ideas while talking to yourself.
- Watching movies at high volume late at night.

A place where you can completely remove social filters and return to your true self. This environment where "freedom of expression" is guaranteed is an important device for boosting self-esteem and releasing accumulated stress.

---

## 3. Deep Work: The "Quality" of Focus as an Asset

What high-productivity knowledge workers seek isn't half-hearted focus, but a <strong>"Flow State."</strong> It is said that it takes an average of 23 minutes to regain focus once it's been interrupted.

A soundproof room physically prevents this "interruption of focus."
Do you spend 3 hours on a 1-hour task, or condense it into 1 hour in a soundproof room? That accumulation eventually becomes an "asset of time" and decisively changes the quality of your life.

---

## Summary: A "Shelter for the Soul"

In this day and age, owning a soundproof room is more than just an extension of a hobby. It is an <strong>"active defense investment"</strong> to protect your own mental state from a world overflowing with information and noise.

If you've been feeling "somehow tired" or "unable to focus" lately, it might not be a lack of mental power, but a <strong>"lack of silence."</strong> Take a moment to experience true quiet in your own sanctuary.
